# Glossary

Each entry gives a plain-language meaning first, then the precise sense used
in this repository.

| Term | Plain meaning | Precise sense here |
|---|---|---|
| **&alpha; (fine-structure constant)** | The number that sets how strongly light and charged matter interact, about 1/137. | &alpha; = e&sup2;/4&pi;&epsilon;&#8320;&#8463;c = 1/137.036. &alpha;/2 in degrees is 0.20905&deg;. |
| **&beta; (birefringence angle)** | How far the polarization of ancient light has been turned on its way to us. | Rotation of the CMB linear-polarization angle; &beta; = (k&alpha;/2&pi;)[&theta;(observer) &minus; &theta;(emission)]. |
| **&Delta;&beta; (tomography)** | The difference between the turn of "late" light and "early" light. | &beta;_reio &minus; &beta;_rec, from the low-&ell; reionization signal and the high-&ell; recombination signal. |
| **CMB** | The afterglow of the Big Bang, released 380,000 years after it. | Cosmic microwave background, last scattering at z* &asymp; 1090. |
| **Polarization; E and B modes** | Light's electric field can prefer one direction. Patterns of that preference come in two types. | E modes are curl-free, B modes divergence-free. A rotation by &beta; turns E into B, producing an EB correlation &prop; sin 4&beta;. |
| **Multipole &ell;** | A measure of angular size on the sky: small &ell; means large patches. | Spherical-harmonic index; &ell; &asymp; 180&deg;/angle. |
| **Recombination** | When the universe cooled enough for atoms to form and the CMB was released. | z &asymp; 1090, 380,000 years after the Big Bang. |
| **Reionization** | When the first stars re-ionized the gas, scattering some CMB light again. | z_re &asymp; 7.7 (tanh model, optical depth &tau; = 0.0544). Its imprint is at &ell; &lesssim; 10. |
| **Redshift z** | How much the universe has expanded since light was emitted; bigger z means earlier. | 1 + z = a(today)/a(then). |
| **Axion / ALP** | A hypothetical, very light particle that can rotate light's polarization. | A pseudoscalar &phi; coupled through g&phi;FF&#771;. ALP = axion-like particle, with mass and coupling unrelated. |
| **&theta; (the STUR-ROLL field)** | The hidden field whose half-turn rotates light. | Holonomy &theta; = &oint;B_y of a Z&#8322;-odd U(1)_B around the fifth dimension; an angle with period 2&pi;. |
| **Holonomy / Wilson line** | How much a field "twists" as you go once around a loop. | Phase exp(i&oint;A) picked up by a charged particle around a closed path. |
| **Hilltop** | A balance point at the top of a hill: any nudge sends the field rolling. | A maximum of V(&theta;); here V = m&sup2;f&sup2;(1 + cos &theta;), maximal at &theta; = 0. |
| **Fifth dimension, orbifold S&sup1;/Z&#8322;** | An extra direction of space, curled up very small and folded in half. | A circle of circumference L identified under y &rarr; &minus;y; fixed points at y = 0 and y = L/2. |
| **Brane** | A boundary of the extra dimension where ordinary particles can live. | A 4D hypersurface at an orbifold fixed point; the Standard Model is brane-localised. |
| **Brane angle &vartheta;** | A switch on the boundary that can flip the way fermions feel the extra dimension. | Orbifold-odd brane angle coupling &vartheta;J^y; its parity-fixed minimum &vartheta; = &pi; is a fermion-parity defect. |
| **Periodic / antiperiodic fermions (spin structure)** | Whether a matter particle comes back to itself, or to minus itself, after going once around the extra dimension. | Boundary condition &psi;(y + L) = &plusmn;&psi;(y). A smoothly capped dimension forces antiperiodic. |
| **Chern&ndash;Simons term, level k** | A special interaction whose strength can only come in whole-number steps. | 5D term &prop; &kappa; B&and;F&and;F; reduces to (k&alpha;/4&pi;)&theta;FF&#771; with integer k. |
| **Parity anomaly** | A quantum rule that forces some quantities to be half a unit off a whole number unless matter makes up the difference. | A 5D Dirac fermion shifts the effective level by &frac12; sgn M, so the bare level is in &Zopf; + &frac12; and k is an integer. |
| **Z&#8326; global structure** | A statement about how the forces we know fit together; it forbids free particles with a third of an electron's charge. | Gauge group [SU(3)&times;SU(2)&times;U(1)]/Z&#8326;. |
| **KK (Kaluza&ndash;Klein) modes** | Heavier copies of a particle that exist because of the extra dimension. | Fourier modes along y; the first hypercharge mode has M&#8321; = 2&pi;/L. |
| **Decay constant f** | Sets how weakly the hidden field couples to light: larger f, weaker coupling. | &theta; = &phi;/f; photon coupling g = k&alpha;/(&pi;f). |
| **Inflation, e-fold N, H** | A burst of extremely rapid expansion at the very beginning. One e-fold is growth by a factor e &asymp; 2.7. | Quasi-de Sitter phase with Hubble rate H; N = ln(a_end/a_start). |
| **Seed s&#8320;** | The size of the tiny random jiggles of the field during inflation. | s&#8320; = H/(2&pi;f) per e-fold; after N e-folds &theta;_i &asymp; s&#8320;&radic;N. |
| **Domain, domain wall** | Regions where the field rolled in opposite directions, and the boundaries between them. | Regions with &theta; &rarr; +&pi; or &minus;&pi;; separated by sine-Gordon walls of tension 8mf&sup2;. |
| **Isocurvature** | A type of primordial unevenness that observations say is very small. | Entropy perturbations uncorrelated with curvature; bounded by Planck. |
| **Tensor ratio r** | The strength of primordial gravitational waves from inflation, relative to density ripples. | r = P_t/P_s; P_t = 2H&sup2;/(&pi;&sup2;M_Pl&sup2;). |
| **&Lambda;CDM** | The standard model of cosmology: a cosmological constant plus cold dark matter. | Flat FLRW with &Omega;_m = 0.315, &Omega;_&Lambda; = 0.685 (Planck 2018) as used here. |
| **TEGR** | A way of writing Einstein's gravity using "twisting" instead of "curving"; it predicts exactly the same things. | Teleparallel equivalent of general relativity; field equations identical to GR. |
| **Pull** | How many standard deviations a prediction lies from a measurement. | (prediction &minus; measurement)/&sigma;. |
| **AIC** | A score for comparing models that penalises adjustable knobs. | Akaike information criterion, &chi;&sup2; + 2&times;(fitted parameters); a difference below 2 is not significant. |
| **eV, GeV** | Units of energy (and, via E = mc&sup2;, of mass). | 1 GeV = 10&#8313; eV. An electron is 511,000 eV. |
| **LiteBIRD** | A Japanese-led satellite designed to map CMB polarization over the whole sky. | JAXA mission; &sigma;(&Delta;&beta;) &asymp; 0.05&deg; [N1 in R2.1]. |
