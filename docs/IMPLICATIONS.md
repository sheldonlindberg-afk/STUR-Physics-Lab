# What it would mean if STUR-ROLL is right

> **In one sentence.** If LiteBIRD measures a polarization rotation of exactly
> &alpha;/2 that happened after the CMB was released, it would mean that a new,
> extraordinarily light field fills the universe, that its coupling to light
> comes in whole-number units, and (if STUR-ROLL is the reason) that space has
> a fifth dimension whose shape was set at the birth of the universe.

This page separates three things that are easy to blur: what a confirmed
measurement would establish **on its own**, what it would add **if STUR-ROLL
is the explanation**, and what it would **not** change. Terms are defined in
[`GLOSSARY.md`](GLOSSARY.md).

## 1. What "proven" would actually mean

No experiment proves a theory; experiments rule theories out or leave them
standing. "STUR-ROLL is right" would mean, concretely, that LiteBIRD (and
follow-up experiments) find **all** of the following:

| Observation | STUR-ROLL value | What an alternative would give |
|---|---|---|
| isotropic angle \|&beta;\| | 0.209&deg;, the same in every direction | any value (generic axion), 0 (no new physics), or a calibration offset |
| tomography &Delta;&beta; = &beta;_reio &minus; &beta;_rec | &minus;0.12&deg; to &minus;0.21&deg; | &asymp; 0 for a miscalibration or a late, dark-energy-like field |
| &beta;(&ell;) shape | fitted by a single mass, 2&times;10&#8315;&sup3;&sup1;&ndash;10&#8315;&sup2;&#8312; eV | no single-mass fit |
| anisotropy at high &ell; | none | patchy for an axion with random initial angles |

Two honest limits apply. First, these observations would equally confirm the
hilltop-axion scenario of Lee, Murai, Takahashi & Yin [3] with c_&gamma; = 1:
STUR-ROLL *derives* that scenario, so the data cannot tell them apart.
Second, the extra-dimensional parts of STUR-ROLL would be supported
indirectly, through the integer and the timing, not seen directly.

## 2. What the measurement alone would establish

These follow from the data whatever the underlying theory.

**A new field beyond the Standard Model.**
*Academic:* a rotation that is uniform for recombination photons but smaller
for reionization photons requires a pseudoscalar &phi; with a coupling
&phi;FF&#771; that evolved between z &asymp; 1100 and z &asymp; 8. No Standard Model
field does this.
*Plainly:* something new exists in the universe, invisible except for this
one effect on light.

**The lightest field ever detected.**
*Academic:* the timing pins m to 2&times;10&#8315;&sup3;&sup1;&ndash;10&#8315;&sup2;&#8312; eV, a Compton
wavelength h/mc of 0.4&ndash;200 Mpc. The decay constant is
bounded to roughly 3&times;10&#8313;&ndash;8&times;10&sup1;&#8308; GeV.
*Plainly:* its "particles" would be between 10&sup3;&sup3; and 10&sup3;&#8310; times
lighter than an electron; each one's quantum wave would stretch from
the size of a galaxy cluster to the scale of the cosmic web.

**The universe distinguishes left from right.**
*Academic:* &beta; &ne; 0 is cosmological parity (and CP) violation in the
photon sector, a signal that has no counterpart in standard cosmology.
*Plainly:* light's polarization is turned one way, not the other. The universe
as a whole would have a handedness.

**A coupling set by topology, read off the sky.**
*Academic:* an amplitude of exactly &alpha;/2 for a half-turn is the hallmark
of a quantised (anomaly) coefficient: the rotation equals k&alpha;/2 with
integer k. This is the cosmological analogue of Millikan's measurement of the
electron charge, proposed for axion strings by Agrawal, Hook & Huang [17].
*Plainly:* the amount of rotation would come out as a whole number times a
basic constant of nature, the way electric charge always comes in whole units
of the electron's charge. That is a strong sign that the effect is
"topological": it counts something rather than measuring an adjustable
strength.

## 3. What STUR-ROLL would add if it is the explanation

These are consequences of the specific mechanism. They become credible only
if the measurements in section 1 hold **and** the further tests below agree.

**A compact fifth dimension.**
*Academic:* &theta; is the holonomy of a Z&#8322;-odd U(1) around an S&sup1;/Z&#8322;
orbifold. Its circumference L satisfies 0.9 TeV &lesssim; 1/L &lt; f/197,
so the fifth dimension's circumference is at most about 2&times;10&#8315;&sup1;&#8313; m.
*Plainly:* space would have one more direction, curled up far too small to
see, and the hidden field is a twist that wraps around it.

**The parity anomaly realised in nature.**
*Academic:* the integer k = &kappa; + &frac12; sgn M combines a half-integer bare
Chern&ndash;Simons level with the half-unit induced by a bulk fermion. Seeing
k = 1 would be evidence for this anomaly-matching structure in five
dimensions.
*Plainly:* a subtle rule from quantum field theory, that some quantities can
only be half a unit off a whole number and must be made whole by matter, would
be at work in the real universe.

**The global shape of the Standard Model's symmetry group.**
*Academic:* k &isin; &Zopf; requires the gauge group [SU(3)&times;SU(2)&times;U(1)]/Z&#8326;
(R3.2), which forbids free fractionally charged particles.
*Plainly:* a measurement of the sky would tell us something about the deep
structure of the forces we already know: that no free particle can carry a
third of an electron's charge.

**How the universe began.**
*Academic:* the hilltop start follows from axiom A1, a smooth nucleation in
which the fifth dimension caps off, together with the positivity theorem and
the parity-fixed brane twist (R5, R6). This is close in spirit to no-boundary
proposals for the initial state.
*Plainly:* the angle we measure today would carry a fingerprint of the
universe's first moment: it began smoothly, with nothing twisted, and the
twist we now see came later.

**A link between two LiteBIRD numbers.**
*Academic:* the seed condition ties the inflation scale to f, giving
r &lesssim; 10&#8315;&sup3; unless f &gtrsim; 10&sup1;&#8308; GeV; if inflation is the
Starobinsky (R&sup2;) model, r &asymp; 0.003 and the mass is confined to
2&times;10&#8315;&sup3;&sup1;&ndash;3&times;10&#8315;&sup3;&#8304; eV (R3.3).
*Plainly:* the same satellite measures both the rotation and the faint
gravitational-wave echo of inflation, and STUR-ROLL says the two must agree in
a specific way.

## 4. What it would not change

- **Gravity.** General relativity stands as it is. STUR-ROLL writes it in its
  teleparallel form, which makes identical predictions.
- **The standard cosmological model.** Lambda-CDM remains the description of
  the universe's expansion, structure and contents. The new sector holds less
  than 2&times;10&#8315;&#8309; of the matter energy.
- **Dark matter and dark energy.** Both remain unexplained by this sector.
  Round 8 tested the two most natural ways the sector could supply them and
  ruled both out against criteria fixed in advance
  ([`REFINEMENT_R8.md`](REFINEMENT_R8.md)). If the nucleation phase is not
  R&sup2; gravity, the dark-matter question reopens for a stable heavy scalar.
- **Everyday and collider physics.** All new particles except possibly one
  (a heavy partner of the photon near 6 TeV, only at the edge of the allowed
  range) are far beyond any collider.

## 6. And if it is wrong

- If the &beta; signal is a calibration error, the test disappears and
  STUR-ROLL is neither confirmed nor ruled out; it becomes unmotivated.
- If |&beta;| is confirmed but differs from 0.209&deg;, or &Delta;&beta; &asymp; 0
  without large-scale anisotropy, STUR-ROLL is falsified. The integer
  structure it relies on would then not be what sets the angle.

Either outcome is informative. That is the point of making a prediction with
no adjustable parameter.
