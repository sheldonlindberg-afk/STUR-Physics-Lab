## Collider bounds on gauge KK modes

8. Electroweak-precision bounds on the KK excitations of W, Z, &gamma; and g with brane-localised fermions: M_c &gtrsim; 4&ndash;5 TeV. See the review discussion in arXiv:hep-ph/0110346 and the references therein.†
9. Combined fit including Tevatron dilepton and dijet, HERA and precision data: M_c &gt; 6.8 TeV (95% C.L.), as quoted in the literature surveyed by arXiv:hep-ph/0110346.† A modern LHC recast is needed.
