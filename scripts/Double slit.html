STUR Double-Slit – Toroidal Resistance Gate

Derivation Sketch – TOR Phase Gate

We model an electron wavepacket traversing a toroidal resistance gate that dynamically splits the trajectory into two effective paths. The electron is treated as a test charge subject to a bounded STUR-modulated force field, rather than an idealized hard-edged slit.

\[ \mathbf F_{\mathrm{STUR}}(r)= \mathcal S(r;L_0)\! \left[ \frac{k_e q^2}{r^2}\hat{\mathbf r} -\frac{G m^2}{r^2}\hat{\mathbf r} +\alpha_s\!\left(\frac{1-e^{-r/\lambda}}{r^2}+\sigma r\right)\hat{\mathbf r} \right] \]

The STUR radial modulator

\[ \mathcal S(r;L_0)=\tanh\!\left(\frac{r}{L_0}\right) \left(1-e^{-r/L_0}\right) \]

regularizes short-range divergences while preserving long-range dynamics. A toroidal pilot phase kick couples to the longitudinal velocity:

\[ g_0(r,v_z)=g_0 e^{-r/\ell_B}|v_z|, \quad \mathbf F_{\mathrm{pilot}} \propto g_0(r,v_z)\sin(\omega t) (\tanh z\,\hat{\mathbf x}-\tanh x\,\hat{\mathbf z}) \]

The accumulated phase functional

\[ \Phi=\int g_0(r,|v_z|)\,dt \]

acts as a continuous which-path phase. Interference on the screen is computed as

\[ |\psi|^2 \propto 4\cos^2\!\left(\frac{\Phi_1-\Phi_2+\Delta k(x)}{2}\right), \qquad \Delta k(x)=\frac{2\pi x}{\ell_w}. \]
