1. Propagators

1.1 Scalar (R-Field) Propagator

The resistance field R propagator in momentum space:

\[\boxed{\feynmandiagram[inline=(a.base), horizontal=a to b]{a -- [scalar] b}; = \frac{i}{p^2 - m_R^2 + i\varepsilon} \cdot W_{\rm hol}(p)}\]
(1.1) — R-field propagator

where the holonomy weight factor is:

\[W_{\rm hol}(p) = e^{-\beta \Omega(p)} = e^{-\beta |p| L_X}\]
(1.2)

This provides automatic UV regulation without introducing a hard cutoff.

1.2 Fermion Propagator

For 4D fermions after KK reduction:

\[\boxed{\feynmandiagram[inline=(a.base), horizontal=a to b]{a -- [fermion] b}; = \frac{i(\slashed{p} + m)}{p^2 - m^2 + i\varepsilon}}\]
(1.3) — Fermion propagator

The fermion mass m includes contributions from both Higgs VEV and wavefunction localization.

1.3 Gauge Boson Propagator

In Feynman gauge (ξ = 1):

\[\boxed{\feynmandiagram[inline=(a.base), horizontal=a to b]{a -- [photon] b}; = \frac{-i g_{\mu\nu}}{k^2 + i\varepsilon}}\]
(1.4) — Gauge boson propagator (massless)

For massive gauge bosons (W, Z):

\[\frac{-i(g_{\mu\nu} - k_\mu k_\nu/M^2)}{k^2 - M^2 + i\varepsilon}\]
(1.5)

1.4 Graviton Propagator

In de Donder gauge:

\[\feynmandiagram[inline=(a.base), horizontal=a to b]{a -- [gluon] b}; = \frac{i P_{\mu\nu,\rho\sigma}}{k^2 + i\varepsilon}\]
(1.6)

where Pμν,ρσ = ½(ημρηνσ + ημσηνρ - ημνηρσ).

1.5 KK Mode Propagators

For the n-th Kaluza-Klein mode:

\[\boxed{G_n(p) = \frac{i}{p^2 - m_n^2 + i\varepsilon}, \quad m_n = \frac{n}{L_X}}\]
(1.7) — KK mode propagator

Full 5D propagator sums over all modes:

\[G_{\rm 5D}(p, p_5) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{i}{p^2 - (n/L_X)^2 + i\varepsilon} \cdot \delta(p_5 - n/L_X)\]
(1.8)

Unlike standard QFT, STUR loop integrals are finite without dimensional regularization or other artificial regulators. The holonomy weight provides a natural physical cutoff.
