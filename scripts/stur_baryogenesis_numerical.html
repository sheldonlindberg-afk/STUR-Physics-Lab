\[\begin{aligned} (Y_\nu^\dagger Y_\nu)_{12} &= y_0^2 (\lambda^7 e^{i\phi_1} + \lambda^5 e^{i\phi_2} + \lambda^3) \\ \Im[(Y_\nu^\dagger Y_\nu)_{12}^2] &= y_0^4 \lambda^{10} \sin(2\phi_1) + ... \\ &\approx 10^{-8} \cdot y_0^4 \end{aligned}\]

\[\boxed{\epsilon_1 = \frac{3}{16\pi} \cdot \frac{10^{10} \text{ GeV}}{(246 \text{ GeV})^2} \cdot 10^{-8} \cdot f(10^4) \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-6}}\]

\[\kappa = \frac{0.3}{K(\ln K)^{0.6}}, \quad K = \frac{\Gamma_{N_1}}{H(T = M_1)}\]

For STUR parameters: \(K \approx 10\), giving \(\kappa \approx 0.01\).

4. Reheating Temperature Constraint

Successful leptogenesis requires \(T_{\rm reheat} > M_1\) so that \(N_1\) can be thermally produced.

From the STUR inflation derivation:

\[T_{\rm reheat} = \left(\frac{90}{\pi^2 g_*}\right)^{1/4} \sqrt{\Gamma_\phi M_{\rm Pl}} \sim 10^{11} \text{ GeV}\]
(BAR.7) — Reheating temperature

Since \(T_{\rm reheat} \sim 10^{11}\) GeV \(> M_1 \sim 10^{10}\) GeV, the mechanism is viable.

Gravitino Constraint

The reheating temperature is below the gravitino overproduction bound (\(T < 10^{9-10}\) GeV in standard SUSY), consistent with STUR's non-supersymmetric framework.

5. Why Matter Over Antimatter?

The sign of the asymmetry (matter, not antimatter) is determined by the sign of the holonomy phases:

\[\text{sign}(\eta_B) = \text{sign}(\sin\phi_1) = \text{sign}\!\left(\sin\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = +1\]

In STUR, the holonomy phase has a definite sign set by the orbifold boundary conditions. A universe with the opposite sign would have antimatter dominance—STUR predicts our universe specifically.
