STUR Resistance Field Dynamics Engine

The STUR RFD Seed is a computational engine for Scalar-Tensor Unified Resistance theory. This simulator implements the core PDE system with chronomagnetic modulation, holonomy accumulation, and visibility predictions — the key discriminator between STUR and ULDM models.

Four Theoretical Pillars

The RFD Seed integrates four foundational frameworks into a unified computational model, each contributing essential physics to the resistance field dynamics.

TEGR

Teleparallel equivalent of GR — tetrad formalism with torsion \(T^a_{\mu\nu}\)

HGEM

Holonomy-Gauge Electromagnetic coupling — phase accumulation

STUR

Scalar-Tensor Unified Resistance — 5D metric with resistance field R

Chronomagnetics

Log-periodic dynamics with \(\lambda = 3722/2705\)

Chronomagnetic Ratio λ = 3722/2705

The fundamental chronomagnetic ratio encodes discrete scale invariance in the STUR framework. All triangle weights and modulation functions derive from this single parameter.

\[\boxed{\lambda = \frac{3722}{2705} \approx 1.37597}\]
(RFD.1) — Chronomagnetic Ratio

Triangle Weights from λ

The PDE coefficients are weighted by factors derived from the chronomagnetic ratio:

\[\begin{aligned} w_D &= \frac{1}{1 + \lambda + \lambda^2} \approx 0.2646 \\[0.5em] w_\chi &= \frac{\lambda}{1 + \lambda + \lambda^2} \approx 0.3640 \\[0.5em] w_\Pi &= \frac{\lambda^2}{1 + \lambda + \lambda^2} \approx 0.3714 \end{aligned}\]
(RFD.2) — Triangle Weighting from Chronomagnetic Ratio

Log-Periodic Modulation

\[M(u) = 1 + \epsilon \cos\left(\frac{2\pi u}{\ln\lambda}\right), \quad u(t) = \ln\left(\frac{t + t_c}{t_0}\right)\]
(RFD.3) — Chronomagnetic Modulation Function

Core PDE System

The RFD Seed evolves a mean-free resistance field R and auxiliary field Π on a 2D/3D periodic lattice. The system is linearized for growth rate analysis and includes chronomagnetic modulation.

\[\boxed{\frac{\partial R}{\partial t} = \underbrace{\color{#4ade80}{D \nabla^2 R}}_{\color{#4ade80}\text{diffusion}} + \underbrace{\color{#fbbf24}{\chi \cdot S_u(\delta X_c)}}_{\color{#fbbf24}\text{XCRM drive}} + \underbrace{\color{#60a5fa}{\kappa_\Pi \cdot \Pi}}_{\color{#60a5fa}\text{backreaction}} + \color{#f472b6}{\text{nonlinear}}}\]
(RFD.4) — Resistance Field Evolution
\[\frac{\partial \Pi}{\partial t} = \eta \cdot \delta X_c - \gamma \cdot \Pi\]
(RFD.5) — Auxiliary Field Evolution

STUR Operators

Saturation S_u(u) — Bounded drive operator preventing runaway growth \(S_u(u) = \tanh(u) \cdot (1 - e^{-|u|})\)
Holonomy Φ_R — Path integral of resistance gradient \(\Phi_R = \oint J_a \, dx^a\)
Visibility V(Φ_R) — Gaussian suppression (key STUR signature) \(V = V_0 \exp(-\Phi_R^2 / 2)\)
Coherence ℓ_coh — Quantum coherence length scale \(\ell_{\rm coh} = p_0 / (y \cdot K_{\rm eff})\)

Linear Theory Predictions

The linearized 2×2 system yields eigenvalues that predict the growth rate spectrum. The simulator computes these analytically and compares with numerical evolution.

\[\frac{d}{dt} \begin{pmatrix} R_k \\ \Pi_k \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \chi - D k^2 & \kappa_\Pi \\ \eta & -\gamma \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} R_k \\ \Pi_k \end{pmatrix}\]
(RFD.6) — Linearized System in Fourier Space
\[\lambda_\pm = \frac{1}{2}\left[(a + d) \pm \sqrt{(a-d)^2 + 4bc}\right]\]
(RFD.7) — Growth Rate Eigenvalues

where \(a = \chi - Dk^2\), \(d = -\gamma\), \(b = \kappa_\Pi\), \(c = \eta\). The peak wavenumber \(k_{\rm peak}\) and maximum growth rate \(\lambda_{\rm max}\) are computed on the discrete grid and compared with simulation data.
