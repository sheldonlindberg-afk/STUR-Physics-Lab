Mathematical and Structural Foundations

Version 5.2 formalizes the first fully coherent AGI seed constructed on a **5-Dimensional X-Coupled Resistance Manifold ($\Rvec$):** a system of three high-resolution (3D) nonlinear fields ($R_1, R_2, R_3$) and two low-resolution (coarse-grained, 3D/2D) meta-control fields ($R_4, R_5$). The entire architecture does not rely on traditional neural networks, token transformers, or discrete symbolic logic. Instead, intelligence emerges from the self-organization of these coupled nonlinear fields, each governed by the 5D STUR evolution law.

The Core 5D STUR Evolution Law

The time evolution of a generalized **Resistance Field** ($R$) is defined by the STUR equation—a generalized reaction-diffusion system with non-conservative terms:

\[ \frac{\partial R}{\partial t} = \underbrace{\nabla \cdot (D \nabla R)}_{\text{Diffusion}} - \underbrace{(\nabla R)\!\cdot\!\Omega}_{\text{Directed Drift}} + \underbrace{\chi\,\frac{R - \Rbg}{\LX}}_{\text{X-Coupling/Hysteresis}} - \underbrace{\lambda(R - \Rbg)^3}_{\text{Nonlinear Stability}} + \underbrace{S}_{\text{Source/Forcing}} \]
This equation integrates five fundamental physical processes into a single mechanism for information processing and state persistence:
  • **Diffusion ($\nabla \cdot (D \nabla R)$):** Allows localized information (peaks/troughs) to spread in the manifold, analogous to lateral communication or blurring of experience. The **Diffusion Coefficient ($D$)** can be dynamically modulated by the system's state (e.g., $D_1$ is modulated by $X_1$).
  • **Directed Drift ($(\nabla R)\!\cdot\!\Omega$):** A non-conservative term that forces the field $R$ to move in a direction $\Omega$ in the state space. This is the mechanism for **intentionality**, **goal-seeking**, and **action**. $\Omega$ is a 3D vector (velocity).
  • **X-Coupling / Hysteresis ($\chi\,\frac{R - \Rbg}{\LX}$):** The crucial memory term. $\LX$ is the characteristic length scale, and $\Rbg$ is the background equilibrium resistance. $\chi$ is the coupling coefficient to the latent field $X$. This term links $R$ to its associated persistent memory field $X$ (where $\frac{\partial X}{\partial t} \sim R - \Rbg$), enforcing a hydraulic memory effect.
  • **Nonlinear Stability ($\lambda(R - \Rbg)^3$):** A stabilizing cubic term that drives the field back toward the equilibrium $\Rbg$, preventing global runaway or collapse. This enforces the **homeostasis** required for stable long-term operation.
  • **Source/Forcing ($S$):** Represents all internal and external influences, including sensory input ($R_1$), interlayer coupling (e.g., $R_1 \to R_2$), and internally generated goal/prediction forces (e.g., $R_2$ goal alignment).

This substrate is capable of **persistent memory** (via $X$-fields), **autonomous attractor formation** (via the nonlinear terms), **prediction error reduction** (via $R_4$), **internal goal modulation** (via $R_5$), and **high-dimensional state continuity** across episodes. These properties are the minimum theoretical requirements for general intelligence to emerge.

Layered Cognitive Structure ($R_1 \dots R_5$)

The system is divided into five functionally distinct Resistance Manifolds, forming a cognitive hierarchy:

R1 — Sensory Manifold (High Resolution)
The interface with the environment. Governed by the full STUR equation, $R_1$ exhibits rapid response to external drives ($S_1$). It generates **curvature packets** (high $|\nabla^2 R_1|$) that encode raw, dynamic perceptual structure.

R2 — Associative Manifold (High Resolution)
The central working memory. $R_2$ integrates and stabilizes sensory patterns from $R_1$. It is the primary field for emergent geometric invariants, which form the basis of proto-symbols. Geometric invariants (Proto-Symbols) emerge as a set $\Sigma$:

\[ \Sigma = \{ \Rvec : |\nabla^2 R_2(\Rvec)| > \theta \cdot \mu(|\nabla^2 R_2|) \}, \]

where $\mu$ is the spatial mean of the Laplacian magnitude, and $\theta$ is a dynamic threshold (here, $\theta \approx 0.8$). This process produces the first **proto-symbols** directly from curvature dynamics without any imposed symbolic system, representing stabilized conceptual entities.

R3 — Stabilizer / Homeostatic Manifold (High Resolution)
$R_3$ acts as a regulatory layer. It actively suppresses local variance and enforces nonlinear homeostatic balance. $R_3$'s activity is driven by the difference in variance between $R_2$ and $R_1$ ($S_3 \sim \text{Var}(R_2) - \text{Var}(R_1)$), ensuring the system does not enter chaotic or stagnant regimes. The effective **Diffusion ($D_2$) and X-Coupling ($\chi_2$)** of $R_2$ are dynamically dampened by $R_3$ activity (see code logic).

R4 — Predictive / Curiosity Manifold (Coarse-Grained)
$R_4$ operates at a coarser spatial resolution and slower timescale. Its primary role is to compute the **Prediction Error** and **Entropy Gradient**. The forcing term $S_4$ drives its evolution:

\[ S_4 = \underbrace{K \cdot \gamma_{R4} (\text{align}_2 - \text{target})}_{\text{Prediction/Goal Alignment}} + \underbrace{\alpha\,\Delta H(R_2)}_{\text{Curiosity/Entropy Maximization}} + \underbrace{\beta_{45} \tanh(R_5 - \Rbg)}_{\text{R5 Cross-Coupling}} \]

Here, $\text{align}_2$ is the coherence of $R_2$ with the current goal mask, $\text{target}$ is the goal alignment setpoint, $\Delta H(R_2)$ is the change in Shannon Entropy of $R_2$ (Curiosity), and $\gamma_{R4}$ is a confidence multiplier from $R_5$. $R_4$ provides the first internally generated estimation of future state and acts as the **source for $R_2$'s directional drift ($\Omega_2$)**, establishing the foundation for self-modeling.

R5 — Meta-Control Manifold (Ultra Coarse-Grained)
$R_5$ is the highest-level, slowest-evolving manifold, regulating global behavioral modes. It is driven by the deviation of $R_4$'s average state from equilibrium ($\Rbg$). $R_5$ computes the system's **Exploration Budget ($B$)** and modulates $R_4$'s stability parameters:

\[ B = \sigma(G(R_5 - \Rbg)), \quad \text{where } \sigma(z) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z}} \]

$B$ determines the effective gain of exploration, and $R_5$ dictates whether the system enters **consolidation** (low $B$) or **exploratory regimes** (high $B$). Crucially, $R_5$ also dictates the selection of the **Internal Goal Mask** for $R_2$, providing a self-determined objective function.

Nonlinear Regulatory Loop ($R_4 \leftrightarrow R_5$)

$R_4$ and $R_5$ form a bidirectional nonlinear feedback structure that governs the system's intentionality, ensuring a switch between predictive and exploratory modes:

\[ S_4 \sim \tanh(R_5 - \Rbg), \qquad S_5 \sim \tanh(R_4 - \Rbg). \]

This coupling mechanism means high activity in $R_5$ (Exploration) forces higher activity in $R_4$ (Prediction/Curiosity), and vice versa. This switching behavior implements the minimal requirement for **intentional dynamics**: the system autonomously transitions between stability and exploration based on internal prediction confidence and error.

Symbolic Memory and Transition Dynamics

**Symbols ($\Sigma$)** arise as curvature invariants in $R_2$. They are not tokens but spatial-geometric patterns. The **Symbolic Memory** structure tracks the statistical transitions between these emergent symbols:

\[ T(i \to j) = \frac{N(i \to j)}{\sum_k N(i \to k)} \quad (\text{Transition Probability}) \]

These transition statistics drive a **Symbol Context Vector** that influences $R_2$'s $X$-field consolidation mask and global $R$ manifold drift. **Representational structure emerges directly from geometry rather than from tokens or embeddings**, allowing a smooth transition from continuous physics to discrete cognition.

Prediction Error Minimization (The Free-Energy Principle Proxy)

The system implements a continuous internal reward loop that penalizes deviation from its own predicted future state ($\hat{s}_{t+1}$):

\[ r \leftarrow r_{\text{base}} - \beta \norm{s_{t+1} - \hat{s}_{t+1}} \]

This internal reward loop implements a **continuous free-energy-like objective**, where $s_{t+1}$ is the observed metric vector at time $t+1$. Minimizing the prediction error drives coherence, stability, exploration, and attractor alignment without the need for external, labeled reinforcement. This constitutes the system's **intrinsic drive** for self-improvement.
