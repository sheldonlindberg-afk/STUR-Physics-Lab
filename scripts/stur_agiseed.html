Five-Layer Architecture

The AGI Seed implements a hierarchical cognitive architecture where each layer operates at a different spatiotemporal scale, mirroring the multi-scale structure of XCRM physics.

R1 Sensory Manifold — Fast dynamics, external input processing (32³ grid) \(\Delta t = 0.005\)
R2 Associative Manifold — Working memory, pattern binding (32³ grid) \(\Delta t = 0.005\)
R3 Stabilizer Manifold — Homeostatic regulation, dampening (32³ grid) \(\Delta t = 0.005\)

Multi-Resolution XCRM

The coarse layers (R4, R5) operate at lower spatial and temporal resolution, implementing the renormalization group flow of XCRM physics. This naturally produces hierarchical control: R5 modulates R4's learning rate and exploration budget, while R4 controls R2's drift direction.
