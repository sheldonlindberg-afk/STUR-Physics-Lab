1. Yukawa Coupling from Z3 Phase Overlaps

1.1 The 5D Yukawa Interaction

In the Master Action, fermions couple to the Higgs through a 5D Yukawa term:

\[\Matt{\mathcal{L}}_{\rm Yuk}^{5D} = \tilde{y}_{ij} \, \Matt{\bar{\psi}_i}(x,X) \, \Matt{H}(x,X) \, \Matt{\psi_j}(x,X)\]
(1.1) — 5D Yukawa interaction

Here \(\tilde{y}_{ij}\) is the dimensionful 5D coupling (with dimensions of [length]-1/2), and the fermion and Higgs fields depend on both the 4D coordinates \(x\) and the extra dimension \(X\).

1.2 Dimensional Reduction to 4D

The effective 4D Yukawa coupling is obtained by integrating over the extra dimension:

\[\boxed{\Matt{Y_{ij}} = y_0 \int_0^{\XCRM{L_X}} \Matt{\psi_i}^*(X) \, \Matt{H}(X) \, \Matt{\psi_j}(X) \, dX}\]
(1.2) — 4D Yukawa from overlap integral [H.7.1]

where \(y_0 = \tilde{y} / \sqrt{L_X}\) is the dimensionless reference coupling.

Derivation: Why the Overlap Integral? Intermediate

The 4D effective Lagrangian is obtained by integrating the 5D Lagrangian over the extra dimension:

\[\mathcal{L}_{\rm 4D} = \int_0^{L_X} \mathcal{L}_{\rm 5D}\, dX\]

For the Yukawa term, this gives:

\[\mathcal{L}_{\rm Yuk}^{4D} = \tilde{y}_{ij} \int_0^{L_X} \bar{\psi}_i(x)\psi_i^*(X) \cdot H(x)h(X) \cdot \psi_j(x)\psi_j(X)\, dX\]

Factoring out the 4D fields and defining the overlap integral:

\[I_{ij} \equiv \int_0^{L_X} \psi_i^*(X) \, h(X) \, \psi_j(X)\, dX\]

The 4D Yukawa coupling is then \(Y_{ij} = y_0 \times I_{ij}\), where the hierarchy comes entirely from the overlap integral.
