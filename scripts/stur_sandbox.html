Why Simulations Matter

Simulations let you run impossible experiments:

• too big (galaxies) • too small (quarks) • too dangerous (plasma) • too expensive (supercolliders) • too slow to observe in real time

How to Use AI for Theoretical Physics

AI assists at every step: 

1. derive dS/dr or more complex fields
2. check your algebra
3. explore limits & asymptotics
4. rewrite equations cleanly
5. build simulations/PDE solvers
6. search for conserved quantities
7. extend geometry (2D, 3D, helical, toroidal)

How to Code & Iterate With AI

The most important skill in modern physics is not coding — it is iterating. You build the idea, AI builds the code, you refine the direction.

Here is the actual workflow used by modern researchers: 

Step 1 — Describe the physics in plain language  
Step 2 — Ask AI to write the first version of the code  
Step 3 — Run it, find what breaks or behaves strangely  
Step 4 — Tell AI exactly what happened  
Step 5 — AI rewrites the math or improves stability  
Step 6 — Repeat until the model behaves

You can accelerate this by using multiple AIs: 

AI #1 → derive equations  
AI #2 → algebra verification  
AI #3 → build simulation  
AI #4 → analyze patterns  
AI #5 → search edge cases  
You → unify and choose direction
