Derivation: Why Gaussian Localization? Advanced

The fermion wavefunctions are Gaussian localized due to the holonomy potential on the Z₃ helix:

\[V_{\rm hol}(\phi) = V_0 \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} \cos\!\left(3(\phi - \phi_n)\right)\]

\[V_{\rm hol} \approx \frac{9V_0}{2}(\phi - \phi_n)^2\]

For the STUR parameters, this gives σ ≈ 0.85 rad.
