C. Key Derivation Summaries

C.1 XCRM Closure Relation

Starting from the effective 4D potential after X-integration:

\[V_{\rm eff}(\bar{R}) = V(\bar{R}) + \frac{\chi^2}{2L_X^2}(\bar{R} - R_{\rm bg})^2\]
(C.1)

The XCRM term generates an effective mass \(m_{\rm eff}^2 = \chi^2/L_X^2\). At thermal equilibrium, fluctuations satisfy:

\[\sigma_R^2 = \frac{T_{\rm eff}}{m_{\rm eff}^2} = \frac{L_X^2}{\chi^2} T_{\rm eff}\]
(C.2)

The coherence length then follows:

\[\boxed{\ell_{\rm coh} = \frac{\sqrt{2}\, L_X}{\sigma_R}}\]
(C.3) — XCRM Closure

C.2 Gaussian Visibility from Central Limit

The visibility is the average of the phase factor over shot-to-shot fluctuations:

\[V = \langle e^{i\Phi_R} \rangle = \int P(\Phi_R)\, e^{i\Phi_R}\, d\Phi_R\]
(C.4)

For Gaussian-distributed \(\Phi_R\) (central limit theorem when many independent fluctuations contribute):

\[\boxed{V = V_0 \exp\!\left(-\frac{\langle\Phi_R^2\rangle}{2}\right)}\]
(C.5) — Gaussian visibility

C.3 TEGR-GR Boundary Term

The teleparallel-Riemann equivalence follows from:

\[R_{\rm Ricci} = -\Tcal + \frac{2}{e}\partial_M(e\, T^M)\]
(C.6)

where \(T^M = T^P{}_{PM}\) is the torsion vector. For compact manifolds or matched boundary conditions, the surface term vanishes and the actions are equivalent.

D. Approximation Validity

D.1 Markovian Regime

Condition

The Markovian approximation (uncorrelated shot-to-shot fluctuations) requires:

\[\tau_{\rm corr} \ll \tau_{\rm shot}\]

where \(\tau_{\rm corr} \sim \hbar/(m_\chi c^2)\) is the bath correlation time and \(\tau_{\rm shot}\) is the experimental shot period.

Bath Mass \(\tau_{\rm corr}\) Regime (for \(\tau_{\rm shot} = 1\) s)
1 TeV \(\sim 10^{-27}\) s Markovian
1 GeV \(\sim 10^{-24}\) s Markovian
1 keV \(\sim 10^{-18}\) s Markovian
1 eV \(\sim 10^{-15}\) s Markovian
\(10^{-22}\) eV (ULDM) \(\sim 10^{6}\) s Coherent

D.2 WKB Approximation

Condition

The WKB phase accumulation formula is valid when:

\[\lambda_{\rm dB} \ll L_{\rm path}\]

where \(\lambda_{\rm dB}\) is the de Broglie wavelength. For typical atom interferometers, \(\lambda_{\rm dB} \sim 10^{-11}\) m while \(L_{\rm path} \sim 1\)–\(100\) m, so this is well satisfied.
