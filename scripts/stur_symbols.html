V. Equation Color Coding

STUR equations use a consistent 6-color system to identify which physical domain each term controls. This allows rapid parsing of complex unified expressions.

The Six Physics Domains
Diffusion / Kinetic

Terms like \(\tfrac{1}{2}(\nabla R)^2\) — field propagation, energy transport

Potential / Relaxation

Terms like \(V(R)\) — energy landscape, symmetry breaking

XCRM / X-Coupling

Terms like \(\chi R\partial_X R\) — internal dimension coupling, visibility

Torsion / Gravity

Terms like \(\alpha R\mathbb{T}\) — teleparallel gravity, TEGR emergence

Quantum / Loops

Loop corrections, path integrals, holonomy terms

Matter / Fields

Terms like \(\mathcal{L}_{\rm matter}\) — SM fermions, gauge fields

VI. Status Classification Legend

STUR distinguishes between claims that are fully derived, those that require external inputs, and those where the mechanism is derived but values must be fit to data.

StatusColorMeaningExamples
Derived Green Rigorously follows from axioms with no additional input MHP, TEGR emergence, Gaussian visibility, energy conditions
Input Required Gold Mechanism derived but requires physical input assumption SM gauge group [input: anomaly-free fermions], 3 generations [input: n=3 flux]
Accommodated Gold Mechanism derived, but specific values fit to experimental data Yukawa couplings, CKM/PMNS angles
Free Red Must be measured experimentally; not determined by theory \(L_X\) (the single free parameter)
Conjectured Violet Hypothesized extension; not yet rigorously proven Some cosmological applications
Honest Assessment

STUR derives mechanisms for gauge symmetry, generations, and mass hierarchies from its axioms. However, specific values (why SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) rather than another anomaly-free group, why exactly 3 generations, specific Yukawa values) require additional physical inputs beyond the two axioms. This distinction is clearly marked throughout the site.
