STUR Warburg Interpretation

In STUR, each mitochondrion carries a holonomy Φ_R = J·ℓ where ℓ is the membrane path length. As cancer metabolically reprograms, ℓ increases (mitochondrial fragmentation), pinning Φ_R near π/√2 ≈ 2.22 — the compactification maximum where V(Φ_R) is minimized and éR diverges. This maps perfectly to the Warburg transition: W → 1 corresponds to Φ_R → π/√2.

Oncogenic pinning: Φ_R_cancer ≈ π/√2 ≈ 2.221 [V minimized → éR maximized]
éR_STUR = EP · exp(+Φ_R²) = EP · exp(Φ_R²) [holonomy resistance formula]
Order Parameter Landscape — Warburg Transition

W is the order parameter (0 = pure OXPHOS, 1 = pure Warburg). The landscape V(W) shows the free energy wells: normal cell sits at W≈0, cancer locks into W≈1. In the bistable region (W ∈ [0.2, 0.5]), both states are locally stable — a hysteresis loop.
