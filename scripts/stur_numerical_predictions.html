2. Coherence Length: Explicit Numerical Values

2.1 Reference Calculation

Taking benchmark parameters:

\[\XCRM{L_X} = 1\, \mu\text{m}, \quad \Quant{y} = 1, \quad \tilde{\sigma}_R = 10^{-2}, \quad \Diff{R_{\rm bg}} = 10^{15}\, \text{GeV}\]

The coherence length calculation proceeds as:

\[\Diff{\sigma_R} = \tilde{\sigma}_R \cdot \Diff{R_{\rm bg}} = 10^{-2} \times 10^{15}\, \text{GeV} = 10^{13}\, \text{GeV}\]
\[\Matt{\ell_{\rm coh}} = \frac{\sqrt{2} \times 10^{-6}\, \text{m}}{1 \times 10^{13}\, \text{GeV}} \times \frac{\hbar c}{1} = \frac{1.41 \times 10^{-6}\, \text{m}}{10^{13} \times 1.6 \times 10^{-10}\, \text{J}}\]

Converting to SI (with ℏc = 197 MeV·fm):

\[\boxed{\Matt{\ell_{\rm coh}} \approx 3\, \text{m} \quad \text{(benchmark)}}\]
(2.1) — Benchmark coherence length
