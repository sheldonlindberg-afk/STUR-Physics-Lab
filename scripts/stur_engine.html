Scope and Status

This framework presents a unified teleparallel effective theory with an explicit STUR resistance field coupled to torsion. TEGR (≡ GR) provides the geometric backbone; STUR supplies the dynamical scalar resistance field \(R(x^\mu,t,X)\) and its gauge-invariant holonomy; HGEM is the weak-field (linear) limit; Chronomagnetics provides log-periodic modulation. No claim is made that Standard Model parameters, coupling constants, or cosmological initial conditions are derived. This is an effective framework, not a UV-complete theory.

After dimensional reduction, STUR constitutes a one-parameter family of effective models, parameterized by the dimensionless ratio \(\sigma_R/L_X\), testable across interferometric platforms.

This is the single discriminating observable that distinguishes STUR from decoherence, ULDM, and standard QM.

Metric from Tetrads

\[g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{ab}\, e^a{}_\mu\, e^b{}_\nu\]

Torsion Tensor

\[T^a{}_{\mu\nu} = \partial_\mu e^a{}_\nu - \partial_\nu e^a{}_\mu\]
Theorem — TEGR ≡ GR Equivalence

The TEGR action differs from Einstein-Hilbert by a total divergence. The field equations are identical to Einstein's equations.

\[S_{\rm TEGR} = \frac{1}{16\pi G} \int d^4x\, e\, \mathbf{T} = S_{\rm EH} + \text{boundary term}\]

\[K^a{}_{bc} = \frac{1}{2}\left(T_{bc}{}^a + T_{cb}{}^a - T^a{}_{bc}\right)\]

This is the geometric object STUR parameterizes phenomenologically via \(K_{\rm eff}\).

VERIFIER Appendix — How to Falsify STUR in 48 Hours

One-page protocol (self-contained, reproducible)

This appendix is designed so a home lab / university lab can run a decisive test without needing any STUR-internal tooling.

What is being tested

The STUR discriminator is Gaussian suppression in arm-separation: \(V(\Delta L)=V_0\exp(-( \Delta L/\ell_{\mathrm{coh}})^2)\). Competing null sources (ordinary decoherence) generally scale with time/pressure/temperature, not specifically with \(\Delta L^2\) at fixed shot time.

What counts as falsification

If, after controlling known decoherence channels, your measured \(V(\Delta L)\) rejects a Gaussian in \(\Delta L^2\) (BIC or evidence) across multiple baselines, STUR's claimed discriminator is excluded for that platform's sensitivity regime.

48-hour plan (minimum viable falsification)
  1. Pick a platform: tabletop Mach–Zehnder (photons) or atomic interferometer (if available). Use stable optics, fixed shot time \(\tau_{\rm shot}\).
  2. Define baselines: choose ≥ 8 values of arm separation \(\Delta L\) spanning a practical range (e.g., 0 → 5 m). Keep all other knobs fixed.
  3. Controls: run at two pressures (or two temperatures) to characterize ordinary decoherence; keep \(\Delta L\) scan identical in both.
  4. Record raw: for each \(\Delta L\), record visibility estimate \(V\) and uncertainty (or raw fringe data to compute it later).
  5. Fit models:
    • STUR: \(V(\Delta L)=V_0\exp(-( \Delta L/\ell)^2)\)
    • Null: constant \(V(\Delta L)=V_0\)
    • Alt decoherence (optional): \(V=V_0\exp(-\gamma)\) where \(\gamma\) depends on time/pressure/temperature, not \(\Delta L^2\)
  6. Decision: compute BIC (or Bayes evidence). If Gaussian is not preferred (or is decisively rejected), STUR's \(\Delta L^2\) discriminator is falsified in that regime.
