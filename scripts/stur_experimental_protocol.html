2. Measurement Protocol

2.1 Data Collection Procedure

Step 1: Calibration
Measure visibility V0 at ΔL = 0 to establish baseline. Repeat N ≥ 100 times for statistical significance.
Step 2: Path Difference Scan
Systematically vary ΔL from 0.1 m to maximum in steps of 0.5 m. At each point, measure visibility with N ≥ 50 repetitions.
Step 3: Time Stability Check
At fixed ΔL ≈ ℓcoh/2, monitor visibility for ≥ 24 hours to verify no time dependence.
Step 4: Mass Independence Test
Repeat Steps 1-3 with second atomic species. Compare visibility vs. ΔL curves.
Step 5: Functional Form Analysis
Fit data to competing models and perform statistical comparison.

3. Data Analysis Protocol

3.1 Competing Models

Fit the visibility data V(ΔL) to four models:

\[\boxed{\text{STUR:} \quad V(\Delta L) = V_0 \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta L^2}{\ell_{\rm coh}^2}\right)}\]
Model A (STUR Prediction)
\[\boxed{\text{ULDM:} \quad V(\Delta L) = V_0 \left|\cos\left(\frac{m_a \Delta L}{2\hbar}\right)\right|}\]
Model B (Ultra-Light Dark Matter)
\[\boxed{\text{Decoherence:} \quad V(\Delta L) = V_0 \exp\left(-\Gamma t(\Delta L)\right)}\]
Model C (Environmental Decoherence)
\[\boxed{\text{Exponential:} \quad V(\Delta L) = V_0 \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta L}{\lambda}\right)}\]
Model D (Exponential Decay)

3.2 Statistical Analysis

For each model, compute:

  • χ² / d.o.f. — Goodness of fit
  • AIC — Akaike Information Criterion
  • BIC — Bayesian Information Criterion
  • Bayes Factor — Model comparison
\[\text{AIC} = 2k - 2\ln(\hat{L}), \quad \text{BIC} = k\ln(n) - 2\ln(\hat{L})\]
(3.1)

3.3 Discrimination Criteria

STUR Confirmed If:
  • Model A (Gaussian in ΔL²) has lowest AIC/BIC
  • Bayes factor for A vs B, C, D all > 10
  • Fitted ℓcoh ∈ [0.3, 30] m
  • No statistically significant time dependence
  • No mass dependence (both species give same ℓcoh)
STUR Falsified If:
  • Model B (oscillatory) has lowest AIC/BIC with > 5σ confidence
  • OR Model C (time-dependent) fits with > 5σ time variation
  • OR visibility depends on atomic mass at > 5σ significance
  • OR Gaussian fit gives ℓcoh < 0.1 m or > 100 m

Compare L and R circular polarization amplitudes as function of frequency.
