\[E_0 = \sum_n \frac{1}{2}\omega_n = \sum_n \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{k^2 + \left(\frac{n\pi}{L_X}\right)^2}\]

From the DHP derivation, the Faddeev-Popov determinant generates an effective potential for the holonomy:

\[S_{\text{hol}} = c_h \int d^4x \, \text{Tr}(h^\dagger h)\]

The holonomy couples to the orbifold size through dimensional analysis. For a fixed gauge field configuration:
