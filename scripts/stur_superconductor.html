Theory Overview

General Relativity with STUR resistance-field modifications

General Relativity (GR) describes gravity through spacetime curvature (Riemann tensor), where matter follows geodesics in curved 4D geometry. The Einstein field equations relate curvature to energy-momentum content.

STUR modification: Introduces resistance fields R_μν that couple to matter flow, creating additional geodesic deviations. Near event horizons, resistance gradients create observable "hover effects" where particles experience additional radial forces before crossing.

Key prediction: Non-zero radial acceleration at event horizon (R = 2M) in GR/STUR, versus zero in pure GR. This is falsifiable via pulsar timing and gravitational wave ringdown analysis.

Teleparallel Equivalent of General Relativity (TEGR) reformulates gravity using torsion instead of curvature. Spacetime is flat (Riemann = 0), but the Weitzenböck connection carries torsion. Gravity emerges from parallel transport failure.

The torsion scalar T equals the Ricci scalar R (up to boundary terms), making TEGR classically equivalent to GR for vacuum solutions. However, matter coupling and quantum corrections differ.

Key difference: TEGR uses flat geometry with torsion. Near event horizons, torsion diverges at R = 2M, but geodesic structure is identical to GR (no "hover zone").

Where GR/STUR and TEGR Differ

1. Matter Coupling:
GR: Energy-momentum tensor couples to metric (Einstein equations)
TEGR: Energy-momentum couples to tetrads (field equations in flat space)
STUR: Resistance fields modify both coupling mechanisms
2. Quantum Gravity:
GR: Path integral over metrics (Wheeler-DeWitt equation)
TEGR: Path integral over tetrads (simpler measure, no conformal mode)
STUR: Introduces resistance-field quantization (distinct from both)
3. Cosmology (Modified Theories):
f(R) gravity: Modifies Ricci scalar R
f(T) gravity: Modifies torsion scalar T
These are NOT equivalent and predict different dark energy dynamics
4. Spin-Torsion Coupling:
GR: Spin (intrinsic angular momentum) does not directly source curvature
TEGR: Spin can couple to torsion → testable via neutron interferometry
Experimental status: No confirmed spin-torsion coupling to date

Experimental Falsification

For Schwarzschild vacuum solutions: GR and TEGR are mathematically equivalent. Both predict identical black hole observables (ringdown, photon sphere, ISCO). The "hover zone" is a STUR-specific prediction, not a TEGR vs GR difference.
Where TEGR can be tested:
  • f(T) modified gravity: Distinct from f(R), different cosmological predictions
  • Spin-torsion coupling: Neutron/electron spin in gravitational fields
  • Loop quantum gravity: TEGR formulation simplifies spin networks
  • Quantum entanglement: Torsion may affect entanglement transport (speculative)
STUR falsification (independent of TEGR):
  • Pulsar timing: Search for anomalous radial forces near horizon
  • Gravitational waves: Ringdown phase discrepancies from resistance damping
  • Accretion disk spectroscopy: ISCO radius shifts if resistance modifies orbits
