Quantitative Predictions

STUR makes the following concrete, testable predictions for ambient-temperature superconductivity:

Prediction 1: Critical Temperature Enhancement

Materials with \(\XCRM{u_{\rm eff}} > 1.5\) should exhibit \(\Matt{T_c^{\rm STUR}} / \Matt{T_c^{\rm BCS}} > 2\). For hydrogen-rich compounds (LaH₁₀-type), STUR predicts \(\Matt{T_c} \approx 400\text{–}500\) K at ambient pressure when \(\XCRM{u_{\rm eff}} \approx 2.0\).

Prediction 2: Geometric Scaling

The geometric coupling \(\Diff{u_{\rm geo}} = \ln\!\left(\sqrt{n_C} \cdot (n_H / n_{\rm anchor})^{1/3} / a_H\right)\) should correlate with measured \(T_c\) enhancement. Materials with \(\Diff{u_{\rm geo}} > 0.5\) are predicted to show STUR effects.

Prediction 3: Isotope Effect Modification

STUR modifies the BCS isotope exponent \(\alpha = 0.5\) to \(\alpha_{\rm STUR} = 0.5 \cdot (1 - S(u_{\rm eff})/2)\). For \(\XCRM{u_{\rm eff}} = 2\), this predicts \(\alpha \approx 0.28\). Measured isotope exponents should match this prediction.

Prediction 4: Critical Current Enhancement

\(\XCRM{I_c^{\rm STUR}} = \Matt{I_c^{\rm BCS}} \cdot \tanh(R_q \cdot S(u_{\rm eff}))\) where \(R_q = \Delta / k_B T\). At \(T = 300\) K, viable ATS materials should show \(I_c > 0.1 \cdot I_c^{\rm BCS}(0)\).

Falsification Criteria

STUR's superconductivity predictions are falsified if any of the following are observed:

Exclusion-1: Critical Temperature Failure

A material with \(\XCRM{u_{\rm eff}} > 1.5\) and \(\XCRM{V_{\rm ATS}} > 0.1\) is synthesized, but measured \(\Matt{T_c} < 300\) K despite passing all STUR viability gates.
Quantitative threshold: \(\Matt{T_c^{\rm measured}} / \Matt{T_c^{\rm STUR}} < 0.5\)

Exclusion-2: Critical Current Reversal

STUR predicts \(\XCRM{I_c^{\rm STUR}} > \Matt{I_c^{\rm BCS}}\) for a material, but measurement shows \(I_c \leq I_c^{\rm BCS}\) across the entire temperature range.
Quantitative threshold: \(I_c^{\rm measured} / I_c^{\rm STUR} < 0.8\) at \(T < T_c/2\)

Exclusion-3: Isotope Exponent Mismatch

The measured isotope exponent \(\alpha\) differs from \(\alpha_{\rm STUR} = 0.5(1 - S(u)/2)\) by more than 20%.
Quantitative threshold: \(|\alpha_{\rm measured} - \alpha_{\rm STUR}| / \alpha_{\rm STUR} > 0.2\)

Exclusion-4: Geometric Scaling Violation

Materials with similar \(\Diff{u_{\rm geo}}\) show uncorrelated \(T_c\) enhancement, or materials with \(\Diff{u_{\rm geo}} < 0\) show \(T_c > T_c^{\rm BCS}\).
Quantitative threshold: Pearson correlation \(r(u_{\rm geo}, T_c^{\rm enh}) < 0.5\) across ≥10 samples
