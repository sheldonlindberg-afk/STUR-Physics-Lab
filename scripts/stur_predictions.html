10.2 Path Integral Visibility Formula

In interferometry, the visibility (fringe contrast) measures the coherence between the two arms. \[\boxed{\Pot{V} = \left|\langle e^{\ii \XCRM{\Delta\Phi}} \rangle\right| = \left|\int P(\Delta\Phi)\, e^{\ii\Delta\Phi}\, \dd\Delta\Phi\right|}\]
(10.3) — Path Integral Visibility Formula
