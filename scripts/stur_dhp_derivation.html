2. Faddeev-Popov Gauge Fixing

Theorem 2.1 — Faddeev-Popov Determinant

Proper gauge-fixing on the infinity helix introduces a Vandermonde determinant that depends on the holonomy eigenvalues.

\[1 = \int \mathcal{D}g \, \delta(F[A^g]) \, \det\left( \frac{\delta F[A^g]}{\delta g} \right)\]

\[\boxed{\Delta_{\text{FP}} = \prod_{i < j} \left| e^{i\theta_i} - e^{i\theta_j} \right|^2 = \prod_{i < j} 4\sin^2\left(\frac{\theta_i - \theta_j}{2}\right)}\]

The Vandermonde determinant vanishes when any two eigenvalues coincide (θi = θj). It is maximized when eigenvalues are uniformly spread around the unit circle.
