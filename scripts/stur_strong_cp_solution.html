1. The Strong CP Problem

1.1 The QCD Vacuum Angle

QCD has a CP-violating term in its Lagrangian:

\[\mathcal{L}_\theta = \frac{\theta}{32\pi^2} g_s^2 G^a_{\mu\nu} \tilde{G}^{a\mu\nu}\]
(1.1)

where G̃ = ½εμνρσGρσ is the dual field strength.

1.2 Observable Combination

The physical observable is the combination:

\[\bar{\theta} = \theta + \arg\det(Y_u Y_d)\]
(1.2)

where Yu,d are the Yukawa matrices.

1.3 Experimental Constraint

The neutron electric dipole moment constrains:

\[\boxed{|d_n| < 1.8 \times 10^{-26}\, e\cdot\text{cm} \quad \Rightarrow \quad |\bar{\theta}| < 10^{-10}}\]
(1.3) — Experimental bound

1.4 The Fine-Tuning Problem

In the Standard Model:

  • θ and arg det(YuYd) are independent parameters
  • No symmetry forces their sum to vanish
  • Why |θ̄| < 10-10 when O(1) is natural?
The Strong CP Problem

This unexplained smallness represents one of the most severe fine-tuning problems in particle physics.
