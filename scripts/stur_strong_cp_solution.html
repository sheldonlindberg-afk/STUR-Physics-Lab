(1.1) — theta-term in QCD

Why Does This Term Exist? Beginner

In quantum mechanics, the vacuum is not unique for gauge theories. QCD has infinitely many degenerate vacua labeled by an integer (winding number). The parameter theta describes tunneling between these vacua. Mathematically, it arises from the topological structure of SU(3) — instantons connect different vacuum sectors.

The \(G\tilde{G}\) term is a total derivative classically, but quantum mechanically it contributes through instantons. This is why theta cannot simply be set to zero by choice.
