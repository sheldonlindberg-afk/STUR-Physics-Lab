1. The Ultraviolet Problem in QFT

1.1 Standard QFT Divergences

In conventional quantum field theory, loop integrals diverge at high momenta:

\[\Quant{\int \frac{d^4k}{(2\pi)^4} \frac{1}{k^2 - m^2}} \to \infty \quad \text{as } k \to \infty\]
(1.1) — Typical UV divergence

This requires:

  • Regularization: Introducing a UV cutoff ΛUV
  • Renormalization: Absorbing divergences into physical parameters
  • UV completion: New physics at high energies (strings, asymptotic safety, etc.)

1.2 Why STUR is Different

STUR's orbifold structure provides a built-in UV regulator through the holonomy cost function. High-momentum modes correspond to large holonomies, which are exponentially suppressed by the Faddeev-Popov measure.

Key Insight
