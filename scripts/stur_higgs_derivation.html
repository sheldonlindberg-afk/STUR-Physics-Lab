2. The Higgs Potential from Geometry

2.1 Coleman-Weinberg Mechanism

The boundary mode A5 = H has no classical potential (protected by 5D gauge invariance). However, quantum corrections generate an effective potential:

\[\boxed{V_{\rm eff}(H) = \sum_n c_n \frac{(-1)^{F_n}}{(4\pi)^2} m_n^4(H) \ln\frac{m_n^2(H)}{\mu^2}}\]
(2.1) — Coleman-Weinberg potential

where mn(H) are the H-dependent masses of particles running in loops.

2.2 Contribution from Gauge Bosons

The W and Z bosons get mass from H:

\[m_W^2(H) = \frac{g_2^2}{4} |H|^2, \quad m_Z^2(H) = \frac{g_2^2 + g_1^2}{4} |H|^2\]
(2.2)

Their contribution to Veff is positive definite at H = 0, favoring symmetry breaking.

The top quark Yukawa coupling gives:

\[m_t^2(H) = y_t^2 |H|^2\]
(2.3)

Fermion loops contribute with opposite sign (Fn = 1), destabilizing the origin.

\[\boxed{V_{\rm CW}(H) = \frac{1}{64\pi^2}\sum_i (-1)^{F_i} n_i M_i^4(H) \left[\ln\frac{M_i^2(H)}{\mu^2} - c_i\right]}\]

where:

  • Fi = 0 for bosons, 1 for fermions (determines sign)
