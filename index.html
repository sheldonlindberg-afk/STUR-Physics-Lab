STUR Physics Lab

Two Axioms. One Prediction. Falsifiable.

A theoretical framework unifying gravity, gauge interactions, and quantum mechanics through a single resistance field. STUR derives the Standard Model gauge group and general relativity from two axioms, producing a falsifiable prediction: Gaussian visibility decay in matter-wave interferometry.

What Makes This Different

Minimal Foundation

Two axioms. One free parameter. Everything else — the Standard Model gauge group, gravity as TEGR, quantum behavior — is derived, not assumed.

Explicit Kill Criteria

The falsification protocol specifies exactly what experimental results would exclude the theory. No moving goalposts.

Testable Now

The predicted coherence length (0.3–30 m) is within reach of current interferometers like MAGIS-100 and AION.
