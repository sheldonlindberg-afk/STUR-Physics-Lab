Derivation record · 1 Nov 2026

Spacetime resists. The resistance is one phase.

Highlighted: the one STUR postulate. Everything else is established physics or follows from it.

When a particle can take two paths, the paths interfere and make bright and dark fringes. If spacetime jostles the phase between the paths, the fringes wash out. STUR proposes that this washing-out is spacetime keeping a record, that the record costs energy, and that the most spacetime can ever record is the amount a black hole holds. Pushed to that limit, the equation lands exactly on a known black-hole law.

ΦR is the holonomy of a connection Aμ around a loop C. Its variance sets fringe visibility (a Gaussian Wilson loop). The postulate identifies −kB ln(V/V0) with entropy recorded by spacetime; Landauer then fixes the energy, and the Bekenstein bound fixes the ceiling. Saturating the ceiling at the Hawking temperature reproduces Smarr's Mc² = 2TS with nothing put in.

The physical picture being formalised

All matter ends in black holes, which evaporate by Hawking radiation until the universe holds almost no energy and almost no information. That empty state is the condition for a universe-wide quantum tunneling event, with no parent vacuum. Spacetime resists that tunneling, and the resistance appears as quarks: folds of spacetime that hold a definite size and carry spin. Folds bind into larger structures, and the cycle repeats.

Two working axioms carry the picture into equations: the universe is holographic (a region's information lives on its boundary), and energy and information are interconvertible at a stated exchange rate.

known physics

Resistance holonomy

Send a particle around a closed loop and it comes back with a phase twist, ΦR. That twist equals the total field passing through the loop, so it is tied to an area, which is where holography enters later. A steady twist only shifts the fringes; only a fluctuating twist washes them out.

ΦR = ∮C Aμdxμ = ∫Σ Fμν dΣμν  (∂Σ = C)

Aμ must be a connection, not a current, for the loop integral to be gauge invariant. For Gaussian fluctuations, V = V0|⟨eiΦR⟩| = V0e−½⟨ΦR²⟩. Open choice: spin connection (spin ½, torsion, TEGR), U(1), or a new field.

STUR postulate

Resistance entropy

The single new claim: washed-out fringes are entropy that spacetime has recorded, exactly ½kB per unit of squared phase spread. It is backed by the proven link between fringe loss and which-path information, but it goes further than that link does, so it has to be tested.

SR ≡ −kB ln(V/V0) = ½ kB ΦR²

Support: Englert's duality V² + D² ≤ 1. A referee will note that for ordinary decoherence by a known environment the entropy produced is not in general −ln V; the postulate claims spacetime is a specific environment.

failed circular check

Energy, ceiling, and the Smarr check

Recording information at a temperature costs energy (Landauer). No region can record more than a black hole of the same surface area (Bekenstein). Push the phase to that limit for a real black hole at its own Hawking temperature, and the energy comes out as exactly half the hole's mass-energy: a known exact law, reproduced without being put in.

ER = ½ kBT ΦR²,   ΦR² ≤ A / 2ℓP²
ER = THSBH = (ℏc³/8πGMkB)·(4πGM²kB/ℏc) = ½Mc²

Equipartition form: ΦR acts as one thermal quadratic mode, matching Verlinde's E = ½NkBT. Smarr: Mc² = 2TS.

known physics

From quantum mechanics to field theory

The same phase works for one particle and for a whole field. How the phase spread grows with the loop tells you which kind of force you have: growing with the loop's edge means charges fly free (electromagnetism); growing with its area means charges are trapped (quarks).

⟨W(C)⟩ = exp(−½⟨ΦR²⟩),   ⟨ΦR²⟩ = g²∮C∮C dxμdyν Dμν(x−y)

Exact for a Gaussian (free) field. Perimeter law: free charges (EM). Area law ½⟨ΦR²⟩ = σ·area: confinement (QCD), σ the string tension.

STUR hypothesis

One area law from quarks to black holes

Quark confinement and the black-hole limit have the same shape: a tension times an area. STUR claims they are one law whose tension changes with scale, by a factor of about 1038. The equation that carries one end to the other has not been derived yet.

½ΦR² = σ(μ) A,   dσ/d ln μ = β(σ) (to be derived)

The flow must keep electromagnetism on the perimeter law in between. In holographic language μ is bulk depth (holographic RG), so this is how ΦR changes with depth: where QFT meets gravity.

known physics

Resistance to collapse

Ordinary lumps of field always shrink to nothing. Add one extra energy term that grows as the lump shrinks and the lump settles at a definite size. That term is a precise mathematical form of “resistance to collapse”, and STUR needs a version of it made from spacetime geometry.

E(λ) = λE2 + E4/λ,   λ* = √(E4/E2),   Emin = 2√(E2E4)

Derrick's theorem; E4 is Skyrme's term. Quantised Skyrmions carry spin ½ for odd Nc (Witten 1983); geons can too (Friedman–Sorkin 1980).

Known physics · Step 6

A fold that refuses to shrink

Without the resistance term, a lump's energy keeps falling as it shrinks, so it collapses. With it, there is a valley: a size the lump settles at. The numbers come from relaxing the Skyrme model on a 4,000-point grid and match the published values.

Falsification targets

What the equation stands or falls on

In order of importance.

Test the postulate

SR = ½kBΦR² predicts ΦR² = 2E/(kBT): at fixed energy deposited, a colder interferometer loses more visibility. First check whether standard decoherence theory already predicts this scaling. If yes, the postulate is consistent but not new; if no, it is a falsifiable signature for atom interferometers (MAGIS-100, AION).

Done: standard theory predicts the opposite, so hotter loses more (ASSUMPTIONS.md). A one-ion experiment that would show it directly is in AUDIT.md.

Fix the connection Aμ

Spin connection, U(1), or a new field. Until it is fixed, ΦR has no definite value in any experiment.

Derive σ(μ)

A flow equation carrying the QCD tension to the Planck tension across 38 orders of magnitude, with electromagnetism kept on the perimeter law in between.

Show the two areas are the same kind

The QCD area law uses loops in spacetime; the black-hole area is a horizon. AdS/CFT connects them, but our universe has positive Λ, where holography is not established.

Quark quantum numbers

Field solitons give baryons. Getting quarks from spacetime folds requires 3 colours, charges ⅔ and −⅓, three generations and confinement. No existing work does this.

One quantity for bounce and barrier

Write R(ρ) and ρc in terms of ΦR, so one quantity sets both the bounce and the tunneling barrier.

Information paradox

If evaporation ends at zero information, STUR must say where black-hole information goes.
