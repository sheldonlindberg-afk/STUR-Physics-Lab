Which of nature's numbers can be calculated from the others?

The Standard Model and cosmology need about two dozen numbers that are measured, not explained. This project is one Python simulation, built only from established physics, with nothing tuned and nothing fitted. It starts from the measured constants and climbs toward the Planck scale. At each rung it asks whether a known principle fixes one of those numbers.

The idea

A physics ladder, climbed backwards

Physics is usually built upward, from small to large. Here the ladder runs the other way. The simulation starts from what is measured at everyday and collider energies, then uses the renormalization group to carry every coupling up to higher energies. If a principle such as unification, a fixed point or the vanishing of a coupling holds at a high rung, it becomes a prediction for a number measured at a low rung. Nothing is added unless the mathematics demands it.

  1. 1019 GeVPlanck mass

    The Higgs self-coupling vanishes 3% off

    If nothing new appears below the Planck scale, the Higgs self-coupling λ can be zero there (Froggatt–Nielsen 1996, Shaposhnikov–Wetterich 2010). Run down with three-loop equations, that one condition predicts the Higgs mass.

    Prediction 128.3 GeV, measured 125.20. Demanding that λ also stops running predicts the top mass as well: 173.5 GeV, against 172.57 measured. See the running ↓

  2. 1016 GeVgrand unification

    The three forces meet only with superpartners

    In the Standard Model the three gauge couplings never meet. Add supersymmetry above a scale MS and they meet at MGUT ≈ 1.1 × 10¹⁶ GeV. Then α is no longer free: it follows from αs and sin²θW.

    The measured α requires MS ≈ 2.5 TeV. One number is traded for another, so nothing is closed yet. See the couplings ↓

  3. 1010 GeVinstability scale

    The Higgs potential tips over calculated

    With the measured Higgs mass, λ turns negative at 5.9 × 10¹⁰ GeV and reaches −0.010 at the Planck mass. Our vacuum is not the lowest state of the Standard Model.

  4. 103 GeVsuperpartners

    Supersymmetry fixes the Higgs mass excluded

    In the minimal supersymmetric model the Higgs self-coupling is not free. Two independent relations, bottom–tau unification and the top-quark fixed point, both give tan β ≈ 1.33. Combined with MS from unification, they bound the Higgs mass.

    At most 102.9 GeV for every stop mixing, against 125.20 measured. That route cannot hold together.

  5. 102 GeVweak scale

    Quark masses and their mixing not calculable by zeros

    Every pattern of zeros in the quark mass matrices was tested: 61,009 pairs of general matrices (none predicts anything, proved) and 83 predictive Hermitian classes. One Hermitian pattern survives, and the exclusions of 80 of the other 82 are proved, not just searched. The survivor predicts |Vtd/Vts| = 0.197, against 0.207 measured. See the textures ↓

  6. 10−1 GeVstrong scale

    Why gravity is weak mechanism

    The proton is 10¹⁹ times lighter than the Planck mass because the strong coupling grows only logarithmically: ln(MP/ΛQCD) ≈ 45. Gravity's strength for a proton is exactly (mp/MP)².

  7. 10−42 GeVHubble scale

    The age of the universe agrees

    From the CMB temperature, H0 and Ωm, the Friedmann equation gives 13.795 Gyr (Planck: 13.787) and ΩΛ = 0.6846. The size of Λ itself stays open: the 10122 of the de Sitter entropy and the 10−123 of the vacuum energy are one fact, since their product is exactly 3/8.

Part L

The Higgs self-coupling, from the top quark to the Planck mass

λ starts near 0.13 at the weak scale and is driven down by the top quark. For the measured Higgs mass it crosses zero long before the Planck scale. A Higgs only about 4 GeV heavier would bring it to exactly zero at MP: that is the critical curve.

Parts I and K

Do the forces unify?

The inverse strengths of the three forces change in straight lines with log energy, at one loop. In the Standard Model they miss one another. With superpartners at MS the slopes change and the three lines meet. The same supersymmetric model then has to explain the Higgs mass, and it can't.

Part M

Which high-energy theory survives every condition at once?

The rungs above assume different things above the weak scale: unification and the supersymmetric Higgs mass assume superpartners, the Planck-scale condition assumes nothing new up to MP. They cannot all be true, so the simulation does not choose. It tests each hypothesis against every combination of the conditions (gauge unification, bottom–tau unification, the Higgs mass) and reports which survive.

Part C

The one quark texture that survives

A texture is a pattern of zeros in the quark mass matrices. Each sector's entries follow exactly from its measured masses, and every unknown left (a free entry, two phases) is scanned, never set. Of 83 patterns that predict anything, one survives:

up   00X/0XX/XXX      down   0X0/X0X/0XX      (rows; X = nonzero, 0 = zero)

Its exclusions of the other patterns are proved, not just searched, for 80 of 82: a branch-and-bound covers every point of every family and both phases with cells, and rigorous bounds on the matrix entries, the eigenvectors and the CKM elements show that no point in any cell fits (--certify; report: results/production_v2_2_certified.txt). The other two stay numerical, excluded at 14σ and 17σ.

The three measured moduli |Vus|, |Vcb|, |Vub| fix the survivor's three unknowns, so it predicts the rest. Its best solution:

Parts N, O and P

Masses, the strong CP angle, neutrinos

Quark and lepton masses not calculated

Koide's closed formula, Q = Σm/(Σ√m)² = 2/3, fits the charged-lepton pole masses to 0.4σ. But pole masses are not couplings at any one scale: with the running masses at MZ it misses by 225σ, and for the quarks by 99σ or more.

Strong CP angle θ̄ bounded

The chiral logarithm gives the neutron dipole moment per unit θ̄, so the measured limit bounds |θ̄| < 8.8 × 10⁻¹¹ (2.2 × 10⁻¹⁰ with the hadronic error). A massless up quark is excluded by 31σ. The axion trades θ̄ for a scale fa: ma = 5.77 μeV × 10¹² GeV/fa. The Standard Model's own CP violation cannot produce it.

Neutrino masses bounded

The lightest mass is scanned from zero. The smallest sums are 58.8 meV (normal ordering) and 98.9 meV (inverted); DESI with the CMB allows 64, which excludes the inverted ordering at 95% in flat ΛCDM. A seesaw with perturbative couplings needs new states below 7.6 × 10¹⁵ GeV, under MGUT.

Neutrino mixing one pattern

Every fixed pattern (tribimaximal, bimaximal, golden ratio, hexagonal) is excluded by θ13 at 39σ. TM1 survives: θ13 and θ23 fix its two unknowns, and it predicts sin²θ12 = 0.318 (0.9σ) and δ ≈ 262°.

The scorecard also tests every other minimal SO(10) chain at two loops: both chains with D-parity unify too low and fail proton decay. Scanning the unknown threshold corrections shows that only Pati–Salam with D-parity is excluded whatever the heavy spectrum; the other verdicts, including Pati–Salam's survival, depend on it. Only non-supersymmetric SO(10) through Pati–Salam fails nothing at this order. It still predicts nothing: its scales are solved from the measured couplings. The entry to watch is the Higgs mass. Moving the criticality condition from the Planck scale to the intermediate scale MI gives 125.6 GeV against 125.20 measured, but the script also shows why that is not a prediction. The heavy scalar at MI shifts λ by an unknown amount, so λ(MI) = 0 is not required. The near hit only says that MI lies close to the scale, about 6 × 10¹⁰ GeV, where the Standard Model's λ turns negative.

Patterns that emerged

  • identityGravity's strength for a proton is exactly (mp/MPlanck)². "Why is gravity weak?" becomes "why is the proton light?"
  • identityThe de Sitter entropy (10122) times the dark-energy-to-Planck density ratio (10−123) is exactly 3/8. The two famous huge numbers are one fact.
  • near missThe Higgs sits within 3% of the mass at which its self-coupling would vanish at the Planck scale, and the top within 1 GeV of where that coupling would also stop running. Either the Standard Model reaches the Planck scale almost exactly critical, or something enters on the way.
  • testableThe one surviving quark texture fixes |Vtd/Vts| from three other CKM numbers, and lands 2.2σ from the measurement. It is the only place where zeros in the mass matrices still predict anything.
  • coincidenceThe smallest mass gravity can entangle within the age of the universe (89 MeV/c²) is 0.64 pion masses. It is the same combination of ħ, G, c and the Hubble time as Weinberg's 1972 large-number relation. No mechanism is known.
  • coincidenceKoide's Q = 2/3 holds for the charged-lepton pole masses to 0.4σ, yet fails by 225σ for the running masses at MZ, the ones that are couplings. A relation without a scale has no known mechanism.
  • testableThe TM1 mixing pattern, one column of tribimaximal mixing kept, survives (θ12 at 0.9σ) and fixes the CP phase from θ13 and θ23: δ ≈ 262°, for DUNE and Hyper-Kamiokande.
  • calculatedAnomaly cancellation fixes the Standard Model's hypercharges and quantizes electric charge, which is why atoms are neutral. In the 3-3-1 extension it also fixes the number of families: a multiple of three, and exactly three if QCD stays asymptotically free.
  • mechanismThe baryon asymmetry, ηB = 6.1 × 10⁻¹⁰, cannot come from the Standard Model alone: its electroweak transition is a smooth crossover and its CP violation is 14 orders too small. Leptogenesis can make it if the lightest right-handed neutrino is heavier than about 10¹⁰ GeV (with the washout calculated), inside the seesaw window and below the Pati–Salam scale.
  • near missWithout supersymmetry, SO(10) breaking through Pati–Salam unifies the three forces with an intermediate scale of about 5 × 10¹¹ GeV. The proton survives the Super-K limit, and that intermediate scale is exactly where the seesaw and leptogenesis need the right-handed neutrinos: one chain that ties unification, neutrino masses and the baryon asymmetry together.
  • near missIf the axion that solves the strong CP problem lives at the Pati–Salam scale, it makes about two thirds of the dark matter by misalignment alone, with a mass near 40 μeV, and strings add more. One scale would then carry unification, neutrino masses, the strong CP solution and dark matter.
  • openStill not calculated by anything: α, the Yukawa couplings, θ̄, the neutrino masses, the cosmological constant, and whether gravity is quantum. The last is a measurement waiting to be made, because a classical gravitational field cannot entangle masses at all.

How it works

Nothing tuned, nothing fitted

Measured inputs only

About thirty constants (masses, couplings, the CMB temperature, G), each with its uncertainty and source: PDG 2024, Planck 2018, CODATA 2018. They are the only physics numbers that enter a calculation.

Established physics only

The Friedmann equation, the renormalization group with coefficients from group theory, exact linear algebra, and semiclassical gravity. Every scale comes from an earlier step.

Unknowns are scanned

Quantities nothing fixes, such as a phase or the stop mixing, are never given a value. They are scanned over their whole range, and the prediction is the band that results.

Checked by itself

A test fails if any tunable number appears outside the input tables. The run ends with 61 self-checks of the calculation itself, which test correctness, not agreement with data. Every coefficient quoted from the literature is checked against a published result.

One model, traced

The parts run as stages of one algorithm. Every read of an input and of an earlier stage's result is recorded, so the report prints the dependency graph the code actually followed. Change one input (--set) and every stage it reaches is recalculated.

Proved where it can be

Where a claim is "nothing fits anywhere", a search is not enough. The zero-pattern results of Part F are proved exactly, and 80 of the 82 exclusions of Part C are proved by branch-and-bound over their whole parameter space.

Run it

Download .py View on GitHub

One file, standard library only. Paste it into any Python 3.8+ runner, notebook or AI assistant's code tool. It runs in about 6 minutes. Expected output: results/production_v2_2.txt.

python3 production_v2_2.py                     # full report, Parts A to W
python3 production_v2_2.py --json-out out.json # every number, as JSON
python3 production_v2_2.py --set M_t=173.5      # change an input; the whole model is recalculated
python3 production_v2_2.py --certify            # also prove the texture exclusions (about 10 more minutes)
