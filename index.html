Measurement and gravity, from the quantum level up.

Highlighted: gravity's own action, in TEGR form. Its strength G comes from how entangled the quantum vacuum is.

Start with nothing but quantum physics: no gravity, no classical world, no collapse. Each branch of a superposition carries its own version of every field around it, including gravity. Whether the branches can still interfere depends only on whether those fields kept a record. Gravity's strength comes out of how entangled empty space is. The same equation explains the double slit, gives back Einstein's tests of gravity, shows that gravity follows each branch if it is smeared at the theory's own cutoff, and predicts what two diamonds will do when only gravity connects them.

Status: a synthesis of known physics with computed checks. Its gravity prediction (each branch, λ = 1) is also what standard quantum gravity predicts, so the diamond experiments test classical gravity rather than this theory alone. Where this stands.

Branch i of the matter sources field states |Ei⟩ (detectors, photons, its own Coulomb field, the tetrad ei). Interference is weighted by the record overlap ⟨Ej|Ei⟩, phases by the action difference including the TEGR field action. G follows from the vacuum area law (Srednicki) and Jacobson's Clausius argument; the Born rule follows from envariance, additivity and no-signaling.

Where the starting ideas ended up

“Measuring creates what we see.” That is the factor ⟨Ej|Ei⟩ going to zero: a definite outcome appears once the surroundings hold a record, whether or not anyone looks.

“The electron is a real EM cloud.” Its field really is in both branches. It is there as an amplitude, not as a charge split in half: a split charge would repel itself, and hydrogen and electron interferometers rule that out.

“Entanglement is one thing stretched across space.” That is the joint state of matter and its fields, branch by branch. It gives the full quantum correlations and, because of the Born rule, it cannot carry a message.

“Gravity is a consequence of the quantum level.” G comes from vacuum entanglement, and gravity then sits in |Ei⟩ with every other field.

The derivation

From quantum fields to the equation

start

D0 · Only quantum physics

States, how they change in time, and quantum fields made of tiny pieces a distance a apart. Nothing else: no gravity, no classical objects, no collapse, and no probability rule yet.

Unitary quantum mechanics with tensor products; locality (no-signaling); non-contextual probabilities; N local fields with cutoff a. Not assumed: the Born rule, a metric, collapse.

derived

DB · The Born rule

Probabilities equal the squared size of the amplitude. That follows from the rest: any other rule would contradict itself, fail to add up to 1, or let you send messages faster than light.

pk = |ck|² = Tr(ρPk)

Envariance + fine-graining (Zurek); Gleason-type additivity; no-signaling selects the power 2. See below.

check passed

D1 · Empty space is entangled

Draw any surface in empty space: the quantum field on one side is entangled with the other side, by an amount proportional to the surface's area.

S = 0.3047 (R/a)² per field

Srednicki's method, R/a = 10.5–18.5, extrapolated in 1/R (literature 0.30). stur_unification.py

check passed

D2 · Empty space looks warm when you accelerate

An accelerating observer sees the vacuum as a warm bath. That is a property of its entanglement, checked here on a lattice.

K = 2π × boost energy,   T = ℏg / 2πckB

Lattice modular Hamiltonian: 0.99969, 0.99992, 0.99997 of 2π at L = 20, 40, 60 (Bisognano–Wichmann).

known physics

D3 · Heat flow gives gravity

Require that heat crossing every such horizon obeys ordinary thermodynamics. The result is Einstein's equations, with gravity's strength set by the entanglement of step D1.

δQ = TδS  ⇒  Gμν + Λgμν = 8πG Tμν/c⁴,   1/4Gℏ = η

Jacobson 1995, re-derived symbolically (Raychaudhuri, the null-vector lemma, Bianchi): src/stur_jacobson.py. The measured G fixes a²/N: a = 0.31 ℓP for one field, 3.1 ℓP for a hundred.

check passed

D4–D5 · Gravity as torsion (TEGR)

Those equations come from a force-field theory on flat space, with the same structure as electromagnetism. Its black-hole solution comes out uniquely, and its weak limit is Newton's law.

Sgrav = (c⁴/16πG) ∫ e T d⁴x,   T = −R + ∂(…)

Identity residual ≤ 10−165; static solution e−2B = 1 + C1/r, A = −B (Birkhoff); weak field Ψ = Φ, ∇²Φ = 4πGρ.

conditional

D6 · Each branch has its own fields

In a superposition, each branch makes its own version of every field around it, gravity included. Whether two branches can still interfere depends on whether the fields kept a record. That is the equation at the top.

|Ψ⟩ = Σi ci |i⟩ |Ei⟩ eiSi/ℏ

For a field made of the quantum level this is exact in a lattice model: entangling phase 0.6064 against the emergent 1/r law's 0.6046, with a record of 2×10−6 left behind. For gravity it is derived only if a statistical gravity would be smeared at the cutoff and be Markovian (below); it is also standard quantum gravity's prediction.

Derived · Step DB

Why probability is amplitude squared

Quantum theory says the chance of an outcome is the square of its amplitude. Here that rule is not assumed. It is the only rule that is consistent, adds up to 1, and can't be used to send messages faster than light.

Assumed instead: A1 unitarity with tensor products, A2 locality (acting on E cannot change S's statistics), A3 non-contextuality. Computed by src/stur_born.py.

Envariance derived

With equal amplitudes, a swap on the system is undone by a swap on its surroundings alone, so the two outcomes must be equally likely. Unequal amplitudes split into equal pieces by acting on the surroundings only, and counting the pieces gives |c|².

Why it matters for entanglementAn instant connection across space is allowed; a message is not. The Born rule is exactly the rule that keeps “one object stretched across space” from becoming a faster-than-light telegraph.
Frequencies, and the remaining caveat

Repeat a measurement with |c0|² = 0.4 N times. The total weight of branches whose observed frequency is off by more than 0.05 is 0.75, 0.31, 1.2×10−3 and 2.0×10−24 for N = 10, 100, 1,000 and 10,000. This route reads small weight as negligible, so it is not free of the rule on its own; the routes above do not need it.

Envariance assumes that probabilities exist and depend only on the state; that step is debated (Schlosshauer & Fine 2005). Additivity and no-signaling do not rely on it.

Interactive · Consequence (a)

A record, not an observer, erases the fringes

Put a detector at the slits. Turning up its strength makes it record more about which slit the particle used, and the fringes fade by exactly that much. Nobody needs to read the detector: the record alone does it.

Detector states |E1⟩, |E2⟩ with ⟨E1|E2⟩ = cosθ. Plotted: I(x) ∝ envelope × (1 + V cos kx), with V = |⟨E1|E2⟩| and D = |sinθ|. The Python simulation (stur_measurement.py T3) gives D² + V² = 1.000000 at every angle.

0: no record, sharp fringes. 90: a perfect record, no fringes.θ = 90°: orthogonal records, ⟨E1|E2⟩ = 0.

Consistency check · Consequence (e)

Einstein's tests, from the torsion field alone

Solve gravity's field equations in TEGR form for the Sun and the Earth, then trace orbits and light rays through the result. No curved space is used anywhere. Every classic test comes out right.

Static spherical tetrad e0 = eAdt, e1 = eBdr: the TEGR equations give 2rA′ = e2B − 1 and 2rB′ = 1 − e2B, hence Schwarzschild. Tests are numerical geodesic integrals of that tetrad (stur_tegr_gr.py), not textbook formulas.

Interactive · Consequence (d)

Two diamonds, each in two places, held only by gravity

Put two tiny diamonds each in two places at once, a fraction of a millimetre apart, and let only their gravity act. If gravity follows each branch, the diamonds become entangled. If gravity is only an average, they never do. Slide λ between the two and add noise to see when entanglement survives.

Parallel geometry, design A: m = 1.5×10−14 kg, Δx = 721 µm, Casimir-safe gap 181 µm, ramps 2 × 1.58 s, hold 0.08 s: Δφ = 2.187 rad at λ = 1. The calculator builds the 4×4 branch state with dephasing e−γτ per diamond and returns the negativity of its partial transpose, exactly.

0.11 is design A's gas collisions at 10−15 Pa (derived collision rate).

Why gravity follows each branch

Gravity cannot be just an average

A force can come out of a quantum medium in two ways: collectively, like sound in a crystal, or statistically, like temperature. The theory decides between them. A statistical gravity would have to jitter, and that jitter would cook every piece of matter in the universe.

A classical field sourced by quantum matter must carry noise. Minimising the total decoherence over the monitoring strength gives k(q) ≥ 4πG/ℏq² at every q: the Diósi–Penrose kernel is the floor (Tilloy–Diósi). An equation of state is coarse-grained at the scale where its degrees of freedom are counted, here the cutoff a of the area law, so R0 = a. Details: COLLECTIVE.md.

collective, like sound in a crystal

Gravity follows each branch

In a lattice of quantum oscillators a 1/r force emerges by itself (within 1% at 8 spacings). Two probes coupled only to that medium entangle, with the phase of the emergent 1/r law to 0.3%. Moved slowly, they leave no record behind; moved suddenly, leftover vibrations record the path and the entanglement dies. Trapped-ion quantum gates work this way.

statistical, like temperature

Gravity follows the average

Then each branch feels the same averaged pull. To respond to quantum matter at all, such a field must jitter, and the least jitter allowed decoheres matter at the Diósi–Penrose rate. Coarse-grained at the theory's own cutoff, that jitter heats every kilogram of matter by about 0.5 W (the nuclei's own size caps it), 6×1010 times the Earth's heat flow. Radiation searches already require the smearing to be at least 0.54 Å, 1023 times the cutoff. Oppenheim's classical gravity has the same floor (OPPENHEIM.md).

What was put in

What is derived, and what is not

Put in

Unitary quantum mechanics with tensor products; locality and non-contextuality; N local quantum fields with a smallest spacing a.

Computed here

The Born rule, the area-law coefficient, the vacuum's thermal structure, the TEGR identity, Schwarzschild and Newton, the classic tests, why gravity follows each branch (given R0 = a, Markovian), Oppenheim's trade-off for one particle, the two-diamond design and its noise budget.

Not derived: the value of G

The measured G fixes only a²/N: a = 0.31 Planck lengths for one field, 3.1 for a hundred. The Planck length is where the counting stops, not where reality does.

Not derived: which particles exist

The number and kinds of fields (the Standard Model) are inputs.

Assumed, not derived

Jacobson's premise for D3: local equilibrium on every local horizon. His algebra is re-derived here.

What it predicts

Gravity's field follows each branch. One diamond of 1.5×10−14 kg keeps its superposition for 3.24 s, and two of them become entangled with phase 2.19 rad. Visibility loss counts the spread in quanta exchanged, so it grows with temperature at fixed energy deposited. Every prediction, with numbers: PREDICTIONS.md.
