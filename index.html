A Theory of Everything

What if all the numbers in physics come from one simple idea? STUR is a framework that tries to calculate everything from a single starting point.

The Simple Version

Physics has lots of numbers — how heavy particles are, how strong forces are. Nobody knows why they have those exact values. STUR says they all come from one thing: the shape of the universe is like a spiral with 3 steps. That's it. Everything else follows from that geometry.
