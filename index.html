Physics Lab

A small set of notes that held up to review — nothing more, nothing less

This site previously hosted a large personal theory-of-everything project ("Sheldon's Theory of Unified Resistance" / STUR). A full peer-review pass — first on the core manuscript, then, in stages, on every derivation document, interactive page, and calculation script in the repository — found that its central unifying claims do not hold up: the framework's foundational object was never actually defined, and essentially every quantitative "prediction" traced back to free parameters tuned after the fact to match known measured values, often admitted in the documents' own text.

Rather than host that content, this site now contains only the handful of items that survived independent scrutiny — standard, checkable physics and math that happened to live in this repository, with no dependence on the unproven framework. See About for the full story.

What's here

  • Maxwell's Equations from the U(1) Gauge Action standard field theory
    Textbook derivation of all four Maxwell equations from gauge invariance and the variational principle.
  • Domain Wall Energy: Why a Symmetry-Breaking Scalar Must Be Complex standard cosmology
    Zel'dovich–Kobzarev–Okun bound worked through explicitly for a real vs. complex scalar field.
  • Zeta-Function Regularization on a Z₃ Orbifold standard QFT technique
    A worked example of zeta-function regularization, cross-checked two independent ways.
  • Can a Dimensional Constant Be Derived from Pure Mathematics? dimensional analysis
    The Buckingham-π argument for why every physical theory needs at least one dimensional input.
