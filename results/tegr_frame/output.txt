Q3  On the plane t = const, x = 0: what the frame is conjugate to (CHECK)
==============================================================================
   s-s part of W^(tx): 0
   8 pi x coefficient of d1 a01: -Cs22(t, x, y, z) - Cs33(t, x, y, z)
   8 pi x coefficient of d1 a02: Ca12(t, x, y, z) + Cs12(t, x, y, z)
   8 pi x coefficient of d1 a03: Ca13(t, x, y, z) + Cs13(t, x, y, z)
   8 pi x coefficient of d1 a12: -Ca02(t, x, y, z) - Cs02(t, x, y, z)
   8 pi x coefficient of d1 a13: -Ca03(t, x, y, z) - Cs03(t, x, y, z)
   8 pi x coefficient of d1 a23: 0
   a_01 = -eta (normal boost rapidity); s_22 + s_33 = delta sqrt(sigma) (area density).
   So W^(tx) contains (1/8 pi) d eta ^ d sqrt(sigma): the frame's normal boost is
   conjugate to area / 8 pi G (Carlip and Teitelboim, gr-qc/9312002, in metric
   variables). The in-surface rotation a_23 pairs with nothing: still gauge.

Q7  What this quantizes to (KNOWN: Varrin 2610.37750, eqs. 4.14, 4.20)
==============================================================================
   A = 100: group SL(2,R) 2 ln mu = 1.376;  semigroup SL+(2,R) = 24.9894;  A/4 = 25.0
   A = 1000: group SL(2,R) 2 ln mu = 5.981;  semigroup SL+(2,R) = 254.595;  A/4 = 250.0
   The group measure gives ln A; only the causal semigroup gives A/4. For Lorentz boosts
   the causal condition is automatic, so TEGR's frame alone does not give A/4. The
   exponential needs the normal-plane edge modes, which the frame does not supply.
