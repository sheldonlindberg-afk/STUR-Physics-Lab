K4  How surprising is the lepton result?  (hostile check)
==============================================================================
   m_tau log-uniform in [m_mu, 10 GeV]: P(|Q - 2/3| <= 5.1e-06) = 2.1e-05
   Trials factor (how many 'nice' formulas and fractions people tried) is unknown;
     with   100 independent tries: chance of one such hit = 0.2%
     with  1000 independent tries: chance of one such hit = 2.1%
     with 10000 independent tries: chance of one such hit = 18.9%
   -> suggestive, not proof.
   Theory status: Koide is NOT derived from any accepted framework; masses run with
   scale, and Q = 2/3 holds for POLE masses only - a puzzle for any field-theory origin.
