F2  Spin from each kind of twist (J = q Phi / 4 pi)
==============================================================================
   (a) electromagnetic flux, Dirac-quantised q Phi = 2 pi n:  J = n/2
       n = 1:  J = 1/2
       n = 2:  J = 1
       n = 3:  J = 3/2
   (b) colour-centre (Z3) flux, phase q Phi = 2 pi k / 3 seen by a quark:  J = k/6
       k = 1:  J = 1/6   (not a multiple of 1/2: forbidden for an isolated object)
       k = 2:  J = 1/3   (not a multiple of 1/2: forbidden for an isolated object)
   -> spin 1/2 needs a FULL Dirac twist (odd n), not a 1/3 twist.

F4  Colour-magnetic folds (Cartan / GNO): is spin colour-blind?
==============================================================================
   weights w_i . w_j:
     +0.3333  -0.1667  -0.1667
     -0.1667  +0.3333  -0.1667
     -0.1667  -0.1667  +0.3333
   minimal Dirac-consistent fold along colour 1: J seen by colours 1,2,3 = 1, -1/2, -1/2
   -> the 'spin' depends on the quark's colour: breaks exact SU(3) colour. NO-GO for the
      minimal colour fold. (Spin-from-isospin, Jackiw-Rebbi 1976, needs an adjoint Higgs
      that breaks the gauge group - QCD has none.)

     d       Q = -1/3  triality 1:  Q - 2t/3 mod 1 = 0
