==============================================================================
P2  Lab-testable consequences of the unified equation (all STANDARD physics)
==============================================================================
  (a) V = <(-1)^{Delta N}> (visibility = parity of exchanged quanta): testable with a
      qubit + cavity mode, measuring photon-number parity of the cavity (standard).
  (b) Entanglement Hamiltonian = local, distance-weighted energy (BW): already measured
      by entanglement-Hamiltonian tomography in quantum simulators (LIT: Kokail et al. 2021).
  (c) Confining string: entropy length-independent, modular energy ~ sigma x area:
      testable in Rydberg / trapped-ion simulators of confining Ising chains (standard).
  None of these distinguishes STUR from standard physics.
