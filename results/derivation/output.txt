D6  A superposition: THE EQUATION
==============================================================================
   Matter in a superposition of configurations i (paths, positions). From D0, the
   fields are quantum too, and each branch i sources its own field configuration
   (EM and the tetrad e_i). The joint state after a time t:

        |Psi> = sum_i  c_i |i>_matter |E_i>_fields  exp( i S_i / hbar )

        S_i = S_matter[i] + S_EM[i] + (c^4 / 16 pi G) int e_i T[e_i] d^4x
        G   = pi a^2 c^3 / (N c_s hbar),   c_s = 0.3046   (D1-D3)

   and what any measurement on the matter sees:

        rho_ij(t) = rho_ij(0) <E_j|E_i>  exp( i (S_i - S_j) / hbar )

   <E_j|E_i> = how far the fields (detectors, light, the electron's own field,
   gravity) keep a record of which branch happened. The phase = the difference
   in action, including the field's own action.
   The step 'each branch sources its own field' is what D0 implies for a field that
   is made of the quantum level (collective emergence). Exact in the lattice analog:
   [stur_emergent K2] entangling phase 0.6064 vs the emergent 1/r law 0.6046; record left in the medium 2.3e-06
