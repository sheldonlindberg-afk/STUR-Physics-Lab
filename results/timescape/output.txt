T3  Verdict
==============================================================================
   Reference CHECK: LCDM, zHD, z > 0.01: Om = 0.333 +- 0.018  (Brout et al. 2022, retrieved: 0.334 +- 0.018).
   1. Matter only, smooth (EdS): worse by Delta chi^2 = +693.  In an FLRW
      universe the supernovae DO demand acceleration; that part is not an artifact.
   2. Timescape (no dark energy) fits nearly as well: Delta chi^2 = +5.1 (official
      analysis, z > 0.01), ln B = -4.1.  Over all 12 cuts/redshift choices Delta chi^2 spans
      -0.5 .. +7.1 and ln B spans -5.1 .. -0.4: LCDM mildly preferred or a tie.
      Beyond the homogeneity scale (z > 0.075): |ln B| <= 1.4 -> inconclusive.
   3. Seifert et al. 2024 (own Tripp standardization, same light curves): ln B > +5.
      The SIGN of the preference is set by the light-curve standardization.  Undoing
      the FLRW-simulated bias correction moves it the other way (ln B -> -8.4), so that
      correction is not what hides timescape here.
   4. Fitted f_v0 = 0.853 +- 0.039 vs Planck-CMB timescape 0.695 (+0.041 -0.051): 2.8 sigma apart.
   VERDICT: supernovae alone cannot tell 'dark energy' from 'averaging artifact'.
   The difference is a few chi^2 units and flips sign with the analysis choices.
