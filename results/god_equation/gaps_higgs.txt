==============================================================================
H4  What this means for the search
==============================================================================
   * The criterion behind front 7 ('consistent to M_P') does NOT produce the Higgs mass:
     applied as stability it misses 125 GeV by several GeV (one loop here; ~4-5 GeV at NNLO).
   * A Landau pole (front 7) is a breakdown of the theory; metastability is not - the vacuum
     simply decays far in the future.  The two criteria are different; front 7's survives,
     but its power to fix CONTINUOUS parameters fails at the first test.
   * Continuous parameters need dynamics with a boundary condition (e.g. asymptotic-safety
     gravity: Shaposhnikov-Wetterich 2010 predicted m_H ~ 126 GeV - LIT, recalled).
