==============================================================================
   meet at 1e+15 GeV needs a_L - a_3 = +4.45 (now -1.50):  +5.95 = 18 extra colourless Weyl triplets (or 36 complex scalar triplets)
   meet at 1e+16 GeV needs a_L - a_3 = +4.10 (now -1.50):  +5.60 = 17 extra colourless Weyl triplets (or 34 complex scalar triplets)
   meet at 1e+17 GeV needs a_L - a_3 = +3.79 (now -1.50):  +5.29 = 16 extra colourless Weyl triplets (or 32 complex scalar triplets)
   meet at 1e+19 GeV needs a_L - a_3 = +3.28 (now -1.50):  +4.78 = 14 extra colourless Weyl triplets (or 29 complex scalar triplets)
   Adding that much SU(3)_L matter moves a_L(N_c = 3) from -6.50 to about -0.9 (fermions), and
   a_L(N_c = 5) from -0.5 to about +5: front 7's N_c-selection chain would have to be
   redone with the new content (not done here).
