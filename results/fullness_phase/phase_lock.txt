==============================================================================
L4  Mutual locking: the system is pulled too
==============================================================================
   system = lock region (R_s = c t)             : dx >= 1.62e-35 m
   1 s lock, 1 m system (small: spreads more)   : dx >= 2.80e-31 m
   1 s lock, causal part of system (R_s = c t)  : dx >= 1.62e-35 m
   Causality: in a lock of duration t only the part of the system within c t can
   respond together, so R_s <= c t and dx >= l_P = 1.616e-35 m, whatever the probe.
   FOLLOWS: the Planck wall is a property of LOCKING, not of light.  Quantum light
   beats the probe wall (L3) but not the back-action wall: the harder the probe
   pulls, the harder the system is pulled.
   (Ng & van Dam, KNOWN, get (l_P^2 L)^(1/3) by assuming the clock is no bigger
   than dx; the causal assumption here gives l_P exactly.)
