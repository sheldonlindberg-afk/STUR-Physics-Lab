==============================================================================
D3  What the bound means
==============================================================================
   Matter degraded since the Big Bang: < t_U / tau = 5.8e-25  (unobservable today).
   Far-future race (yr):  stars stop forming ~1e14 | degradation ~2e+34+ | solar-mass
   black hole evaporates (Hawking, 5120 pi G^2 M^3 / hbar c^4) ~2e+67.
   FOLLOWS: degradation erases all free matter long before black holes evaporate.
   FOLLOWS: it cannot empty a black hole.  Inside the core, rest mass becomes
   radiation, but gravity counts all energy: phi stays 1 and the mass M is unchanged.
   Only the expansion of space dilutes radiation (rho_r ~ a^-4) towards 0.  So the
   user's arc 'to 1, then back to 0' needs BOTH: degradation turns matter into
   radiation, expansion takes radiation to 0.
   OPEN: the theory has not yet fixed tau.  If it predicts tau, Hyper-Kamiokande
   (sensitivity ~1e35 yr, recalled) can test it.
