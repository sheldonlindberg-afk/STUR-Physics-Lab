T1  M2 as a REAL charge cloud (Schrodinger's 1926 charge-density reading)
==============================================================================
   KNOWN: a classical charge distribution has self-energy J = 1/2 int rho phi.
   Standard 1s (no self-repulsion): E =  -13.6054 eV, <r> = 1.5000 a0
   Self-energy of that 1s cloud:     J = 5/16 Ha = 8.5036 eV  (62% of the binding energy)
   FOLLOWS, self-consistent real cloud: E =   -6.6384 eV, <r> = 2.5040 a0
   (variational check, exponential trial: E = -121/512 Ha = -6.4308 eV)
   LIT measured ionization energy: 13.598434 eV
   -> a real self-repelling cloud binds by 6.64 eV, a factor 2.0 too weak,
      and is 1.7x too large. Every line of the hydrogen spectrum would move.
   -> Whatever surrounds the proton does NOT repel itself. It is not a real charge
      cloud; it is an amplitude for where the ONE whole charge e will be found.
   KNOWN corollary: in a double slit every detection registers a whole e, never e/2.

T2  M2: is the electron's cloud made by the strong force?
==============================================================================
   KNOWN: electrons are leptons; they carry no colour charge, so gluons do not
   couple to them at tree level. Test the claim anyway, by range and by a clean atom.
   Strong-force range hbar/(m_pi c) = 1.4138 fm;  Bohr radius = 52917.7 fm
   Yukawa suppression at the cloud's size: exp(-37429) = 10^-16255
   Positronium (e+ e-) has an electron cloud and NO quarks or gluons anywhere.
   Bohr formula with reduced mass m_e/2, pure EM: 1S-2S = 1,233,690,735.4 MHz
   LIT measured:                                  1S-2S = 1,233,607,216.4 MHz
   Relative difference 6.8e-05, which is the size of the known QED (alpha^2) corrections;
   with those included the agreement is at the 10^-9 level (LIT).
   -> The cloud exists, at the right size and energy, where there is no strong
      force at all. M2's strong-force origin is excluded.

T3  M1: the double slit with a which-path record (the measurement equation)
==============================================================================
   KNOWN (Wootters-Zurek 1979, Englert 1996): with detector states |E1>, |E2>,
     P(y) = |psi1|^2 + |psi2|^2 + 2 Re[psi1 psi2* <E2|E1>],   V = |<E1|E2>|,
     D^2 + V^2 <= 1, with equality for pure detector states.
   theta/pi   D (which-path)   V (simulated)   D^2+V^2
      0.00         0.0000          1.0000    1.000000
      0.10         0.3090          0.9511    1.000000
      0.20         0.5878          0.8090    1.000000
      0.30         0.8090          0.5878    1.000000
      0.40         0.9511          0.3090    1.000000
      0.50         1.0000          0.0000    1.000000
   Real phaseless cloud, NO detector at all: V = 0.0000
   LIT: single electrons, one at a time, build up fringes (Tonomura 1989; Bach
   2013). At 50 keV the fringe spacing follows lambda = h/p = 5.355 pm, which
   uses the electron's mass: what passes the slits already has m_e and e.
   -> A phaseless cloud gives V = 0 and is excluded. What M1 gets right: a definite
      PATH does not exist until a record of it exists. The fringes fade continuously
      with the overlap <E1|E2>, not with a person looking. Measurement creates the
      definite outcome (the dot), not the electron (its mass, charge and spin).

T4  M3: entanglement as one particle stretched across space, reacting instantly
==============================================================================
   KNOWN quantum prediction (singlet):          S = 2.8284  (2 sqrt2 = 2.8284)
   Stretched object, instant reaction (MC):     S = 2.8219
   Stretched object, no instant reaction (MC):  S = 2.0000  (Bell bound 2)
   Bob's P(+1) with Alice at a: 0.5000;  at a': 0.5000  -> no message can be sent
   Entanglement swapping: particles 1 and 4 never interact; after a Bell
   measurement on 2,3 (prob 0.25), (1,4) give S = 2.8284
   LIT: loophole-free Bell tests (Hensen 2015; Giustina 2015; Shalm 2015) see S > 2;
   the reaction, if there is one, is faster than 10^4 c (Salart 2008), and any finite
   speed would allow signalling (Bancal 2012), so it must be truly instant.
   -> M3 SURVIVES, but only in its instant form, and it is not new: an instant
      reaction across a single extended object reproduces the quantum prediction
      exactly (it is collapse / Bohm-type nonlocality). It is not an alternative to
      entanglement; it is a picture of it. It makes no different prediction, and
      swapping shows the 'one object' can be assembled after the fact, by a
      measurement, from pieces that never met.

==============================================================================
VERDICT
==============================================================================
   M1  partly right, reworded: measurement creates the definite outcome, not the
       particle. Equation: V = |<E1|E2>| (KNOWN). Fringes need no observer, only
       the absence of a record.
   M2  excluded twice: a real cloud repels itself (hydrogen binding off by x2), and the
       cloud exists in positronium where there is no strong force.
   M3  consistent with data only as instant nonlocality, which is already standard
       quantum mechanics in another picture. Its quantum-gravity version is the
       ER = EPR conjecture (Maldacena-Susskind 2013), also untested.
   The quantum-gravity test that touches all three: gravity-induced entanglement
   (Bose et al.; Marletto-Vedral 2017). If gravity can entangle two masses, the
   'stretched object' must be stretched by gravity itself.
