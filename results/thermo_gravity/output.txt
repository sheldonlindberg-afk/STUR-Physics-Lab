Q3  The heating that noise would cause, per kilogram of any matter
==============================================================================
   R0                  point nuclei        carbon nuclei of finite size (2.75 fm)
   R0 = a (N = 1)      7.8e+60 W/kg       5.09e-01 W/kg
   R0 = a (N = 1000)   2.5e+56 W/kg       5.09e-01 W/kg
   R0 = 0.54 Angstrom  6.3e-15 W/kg       6.30e-15 W/kg
   The point-nucleus figure (1e56-1e60 W/kg at R0 = a) ignores the nucleus's size. With
   it, smearing at R0 = a gives 0.51 W/kg. At 0.54 Angstrom (the bound from radiation,
   LIT) the two formulas agree.
   Required R0 / a (N = 1000): 3.4e+23
   For scale: the Earth's internal heat flow is 7.9e-12 W/kg (LIT), so the
   cutoff-smeared noise would heat matter 6e+10 times faster than anything observed.
   So gravity cannot be a Markovian statistical field coarse-grained at the framework's own
   cutoff. On those two conditions (R0 = a, Markovian) its field follows each branch:
   lambda = 1, and D6 follows from D1-D3. With R0 put in by hand, Q4 applies.
