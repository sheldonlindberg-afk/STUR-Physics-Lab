H3  Acceleration noise: random phases shot to shot
==============================================================================
   Phase noise that cuts the entanglement to 10%: independent 0.710 rad, common 1.319 rad, anti-correlated 1.319 rad
   Phase per unit acceleration, (m dx / hbar) tau = 5.93e+16 rad per m/s^2
   Required acceleration noise (independent, white): ASD < 2.7e-17 m s^-2 /sqrt(Hz)
   LIT LISA Pathfinder, best free-fall test mass ever: 1.74e-15 m s^-2 /sqrt(Hz)
   -> 65 times quieter than LISA Pathfinder for independent noise;
      if the noise is common to both diamonds: ASD < 5.0e-17 (35x).
      Common noise is tolerated somewhat better (it leaves the |01>-|10> coherence
      intact) but is still a random local phase on each diamond. The real cure is
      to measure and subtract it, or to let it cancel inside each interferometer.

H5b  The gas-collision rate, derived (it decides design A)
==============================================================================
   For a split much larger than the body and than the gas wavelength, each collision resolves
   the branches, so Gamma = n <v> sigma_tot. Exact quantum hard-sphere cross-section:
      kR       sigma_tot / (pi R^2)
        100    2.0909
       1000    2.0199
       3000    2.0096
   N2 at 0.15 K on design A's 0.999-um diamond: kR = 4159, so sigma_tot = 2 pi R^2 (extinction paradox).
   He at 0.15 K on design A's 0.999-um diamond: kR = 1572, so sigma_tot = 2 pi R^2 (extinction paradox).
   The interpolation used before (LIT: Romero-Isart 2011, eq. 27, gamma = lambda_th^2 Lambda) is

H6  EM forces that CAN fake entanglement (unlike noise, they are interactions)
==============================================================================
   One electron charge on each diamond: Coulomb/gravity entangling phase = 7.4e+08
   -> both diamonds must carry EXACTLY zero net charge. LIT: levitated microspheres
      have been discharged to zero net charge (Moore, Rider & Gratta, PRL 113, 251801 (2014)).
   Permanent electric dipole, worst orientation: < 1.4e-28 C m = 8.7e-04 e*um for EM < 10% of gravity
   (Patch potentials and trapped charges in diamond typically give far larger dipoles;
    a conducting shield between the diamonds, LIT van de Kamp et al. PRA 102, 062807
    (2020), screens EM while leaving gravity untouched.)
   -> A positive result means gravity only after charge = 0, dipoles bounded or
      screened, and Casimir subtracted. Controls: run with the shield, with
      deliberately charged diamonds, and at several gaps (gravity 1/r, dipole 1/r^3).

==============================================================================
VERDICT
==============================================================================
   1. Nothing in the physics forbids the test. At the published targets (N2) the best
      design needs 270 runs, 0.5 h, and settles lambda = 0 vs 1.
      Margin: with helium as the leftover gas 270 runs; at 10x worse vacuum none.
   2. The split field (lambda = 0) predicts exactly zero gravitational entanglement.
      Noise cannot create entanglement, it can only hide it. EM interactions CAN create
      it, so charge must be zero and dipoles screened before a positive witness
      means gravity (H6).
   3. What binds: mass in superposition (x2e+09 beyond today), vacuum,
      acceleration noise (x57 below LISA Pathfinder), spin coherence
      (> 1.4 s), internal temperature (< 1.89 K), all at once.
   4. The question 'is gravity's field split, or in two places?' is open, and what
      stands between it and an answer is engineering, not theory.
