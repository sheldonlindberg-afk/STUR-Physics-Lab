==============================================================================
G4  The Casimir background (KNOWN EM between the two diamonds)
==============================================================================
   linear  : at gap 200 um, Casimir/gravity entangling phase = 5.8e-02; Casimir < 10% of gravity needs gap > 181 um
   parallel: at gap 200 um, Casimir/gravity entangling phase = 6.0e-02; Casimir < 10% of gravity needs gap > 181 um
   Casimir falls as 1/r^7 against gravity's 1/r, so a modest gap removes it, and
   what remains is a known, calculable EM term to subtract.

==============================================================================
G5  Self-gravity of one split mass (the gravity version of T5)
==============================================================================
   Split-field picture: halves of one diamond attract across dx = 250 um.
   Relative displacement after 2.5 s: 3.34e-17 m  (an atomic nucleus is ~1e-15 m)
   FOLLOWS: unlike the electron's e/2 + e/2 repulsion in T5, a split mass's
   self-pull is far too weak to see. So for gravity, the self-interaction route is
   closed and the entanglement test is the only one.

==============================================================================
VERDICT
==============================================================================
   The 'split field' picture (lambda = 0) makes a sharp prediction: NO gravitational
   entanglement at any mass, gap or time. Standard quantum gravity at Newtonian order
   (lambda = 1) predicts dphi = 0.59 rad for the parallel baseline: ~433 runs at 3 sigma.
   The experiment measures lambda directly: a single number with no exotic physics,
   only Newton's 1/r and the two pictures of what a field does when its source is in
   two places. For EM, experiment already shows lambda = 1 behaviour (T5, Sonnentag & Hasselbach).
   For gravity, nobody knows yet.
