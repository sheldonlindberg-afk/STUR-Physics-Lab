      exchanges left- and right-handed quarks: not possible with SU(3)_L acting on one side
      only (E2). It needs a left-right extension of the gauge group, which is outside 3-3-1.

PART F. DOES ANY TEXTURE OF GENERAL MASS MATRICES PREDICT THE CKM MATRIX?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Every zero pattern of two general 3x3 mass matrices. Physical parameters = exact rank of
  the Jacobian of 10 weak-basis invariants of H = M M^dag (6 masses, 4 mixing invariants).
    61009 ordered pairs of patterns with three nonzero masses, 497 classes
       19 cannot mix all three families (measured: all nine |V_ij| nonzero)
        6 have no phase left, so J = 0 (measured: J = 3.12e-5)
      472 candidates; physical parameters (rank) per class: rank 10: 470, rank 8: 2
      472 of these ranks proved (exact lower bound = symmetry upper bound), 0 open
        2 of the classes with rank below 10 have J = 0 identically (symbolic expansion)
        0 make a prediction and allow CP violation
  RESULT (proved): no texture-zero pattern of general quark mass matrices predicts
    an equality. Every viable pattern has exactly as many physical parameters as there
    are observables (10), so zeros alone, however a symmetry makes them, fix nothing.
    Scope: this is a statement about relations. A rank-10 pattern can still fail to
    reach the measured values (an inequality); that is not tested here.

PART G. OTHER GAUGE FRAMEWORKS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Standard Model with any number of Higgs doublets: an abelian symmetry makes zeros in
    general matrices; Part F: none of those predicts anything.
  Left-right symmetric model with parity: Yukawa matrices are Hermitian, but every
    bidoublet feeds both sectors (M_u and M_d mix the same couplings through its two
    vevs), so zeros sit in the same places in up and down: parallel textures.
    Part C tested 3 parallel Hermitian classes with a prediction; 0 survive at every scale.
  RESULT: texture zeros calculate no CKM number in the Standard Model, in left-right
    models or in 3-3-1, with abelian flavour symmetries.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Hermitian textures (B, C): one pattern survives, with one prediction.
  * Inside 3-3-1 no symmetry can make mass matrices Hermitian (E2), and with its own
    scalars no abelian symmetry makes a usable zero (E1).
  * Zeros in general matrices, the only kind a symmetry inside 3-3-1 can make, never
    predict anything (F).
  * In the Standard Model and in left-right models the same holds (G).
  So with abelian flavour symmetries and any number of scalars, in all three frameworks, the quark
  sector closes with 10 measured inputs: 6 masses and 4 CKM parameters. None of the
  four CKM numbers can be calculated from zeros. Calculating them needs relations
  between nonzero entries (non-abelian flavour symmetries or grand unification),
  which this script does not search.

PART O. THE STRONG CP ANGLE THETA-BAR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  QCD allows theta (g^2/32 pi^2) G G~, which violates P and CP. Only theta-bar = theta - arg det(M_u M_d)
  is physical, and the Standard Model gives it no value. It would give the neutron an electric
  dipole moment.
  O1. d_n per unit theta-bar, leading chiral logarithm (Crewther, Di Vecchia, Veneziano, Witten 1979):
      g0 = delta m_N m* theta/(m-bar eps), from the strong part of m_n - m_p (de Vries et al. 2015);
      d_n = e g_piNN [g0/(2 F_pi)] ln(m_N/m_pi) / (4 pi^2 m_N), g_piNN = g_A m_N/F_pi (Goldberger-Treiman)
      eps = (m_d - m_u)/(m_d + m_u) = 0.370; g0/(2 F_pi) = (15.5 +/- 1.4) x 10^-3 theta-bar; g_piNN = 13.02; ln(m_N/m_pi) = 1.907
      d_n = (2.05 +/- 0.18) x 10^-16 theta-bar e cm (input errors; the counterterm beside the log is unknown)
      QCD sum rules (Pospelov-Ritz)    (2.40 +/- 1.00) x 10^-16 theta-bar e cm
      lattice QCD (Dragos et al.)      (1.52 +/- 0.71) x 10^-16 theta-bar e cm
      measured: |d_n| < 1.8e-26 e cm (90% CL)  ->  |theta-bar| < 8.8e-11 (chiral log); < 2.2e-10 with the
      weakest coefficient above, each lowered by its 1 sigma error
  O2. The explanations proposed, each tested:
      a. massless up quark (theta-bar unphysical): m_u = 2.16 +/- 0.07 MeV, 31 sigma from zero;
         m_u/m_d = 0.460 +/- 0.016. Excluded.
      b. axion (Peccei-Quinn): theta-bar relaxes to zero, at the cost of a new scale f_a.
         m_a f_a = sqrt(z)/(1 + z) m_pi0 F_pi = 5772 +/- 52 MeV^2: m_a = 5.77 +/- 0.05 micro-eV x (1e12 GeV/f_a)
         (leading order; NLO with lattice input: 5.69 +/- 0.05, +1.4 %). At the algorithm's scales:
           f_a = M_GUT (Part I)  = 1.15e+16 GeV  ->  m_a = 5.04e-10 eV
           f_a = M_P (Part H1)   = 1.22e+19 GeV  ->  m_a = 4.73e-13 eV
         One unknown (f_a) replaces one (theta-bar): a parameter trade, not a calculation.
      c. parity: if a symmetry makes M_u and M_d Hermitian and sets theta = 0, then det M is real
         and arg det(M_u M_d) is 0 or pi. Flipping the sign of a sector's matrix keeps V (same
         eigenvectors) and flips its det (3 x 3), so theta-bar = 0 at tree level is always reachable.
         Part C survivor (up 00X/0XX/XXX, down 0X0/X0X/0XX): eigenvalue signs up +-+, down +-+; arg det(M_u M_d) = 0
         as found (heaviest eigenvalue positive), so theta-bar = 0 needs no sign flip.
         But Hermiticity needs a left-right parity (Part E), and the textures that parity gives are
         excluded (Part G); radiative corrections then move theta-bar off zero. Not a calculation either.
  O3. Can the SM's own CP violation make theta-bar? Only through the flavour invariant
      Im det[H_u, H_d] = 2 J prod_(i<j) (y_ui^2 - y_uj^2)(y_di^2 - y_dj^2), H = Y Y^dagger, which
      vanishes if any two same-charge quarks were degenerate. At m_t(m_t), with the measured J: 2.9e-24.
      Its first divergent contribution to theta-bar is at seven loops (Ellis-Gaillard 1979), so the
      SM's own theta-bar lies many orders below the bound 9e-11.
  RESULT: theta-bar is bounded, |theta-bar| < 8.8e-11 (up to 2.2e-10 with the hadronic uncertainty),
    but not calculated. The massless-up-quark solution is excluded; the axion trades theta-bar for
    f_a and predicts m_a f_a; parity sets it to zero at tree level but needs Hermitian textures,
    and the predictive ones are excluded. Theta-bar is the one free number whose smallness the
    Standard Model's own CP violation cannot explain.

PART R. WHAT ANOMALY CANCELLATION FIXES (exact)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A chiral gauge theory is consistent only if its gauge anomalies cancel: exact conditions on the
  charges, and the one place where the SM's quantum numbers are calculated, not measured.
  R1. One family with the SM's representations and unknown hypercharges Y_Q, Y_u, Y_d, Y_L, Y_e:
      [SU(3)]^2 U(1), [SU(2)]^2 U(1), [gravity]^2 U(1), U(1)^3. The three linear ones give Y_L, Y_d,
      Y_e; U(1)^3 is then a quadratic, solved exactly (Y_Q = 1/6 sets the normalization):
        Y(Q) = 1/6, Y(u^c) = -2/3, Y(d^c) = 1/3, Y(L) = -1/2, Y(e^c) = 1
        Y(Q) = 1/6, Y(u^c) = 1/3, Y(d^c) = -2/3, Y(L) = -1/2, Y(e^c) = 1
      the two differ only by naming u^c and d^c. A branch with Y_Q = 0 also cancels (True), but only the
      right-handed quarks are charged and no Yukawa coupling exists (False).
      With Yukawa couplings for every fermion: the SM's hypercharges, uniquely.
      Electric charge Q = T3 + Y/(2 Y_H): u 2/3, d -1/3, e -1, nu 0. Proton + electron: 0;
      neutron: 0. Charge is quantized: atoms are neutral because the anomalies cancel.
      With a right-handed neutrino, SM + eps (B - L) cancels for every eps (True) and keeps
      a Dirac Yukawa (True): hydrogen stays neutral (0) but the neutron gets charge 1 x eps.
      A Majorana mass for nu^c (the seesaw of Part P) needs Y(nu^c) = 0, so eps = 0: quantization returns.
  R2. Witten's SU(2) anomaly: 4 doublets per family (3 colours of Q, plus L): even, consistent.
  R3. 3-3-1 (Part D): [SU(3)_L]^3 = #triplets - #antitriplets = 0 for Part D's 3 families (1 quark family in a triplet).
      With N_f families of lepton triplets and k quark families in triplets (N_c colours), it cancels
      only if N_f (N_c - 1) = 2 N_c k. For N_c = 3:
        N_f = 3, k = 1: QCD b0 = 5 (asymptotically free)
        N_f = 6, k = 2: QCD b0 = -1 (not asymptotically free)
        N_f = 9, k = 3: QCD b0 = -7 (not asymptotically free)
  RESULT: the SM's hypercharges and the quantization of electric charge
    follow from anomaly cancellation and the Yukawa couplings: calculated,
    not measured. A right-handed neutrino leaves one free direction, closed
    by a Majorana mass. In 3-3-1 the number of families must be a multiple of
    the number of colours, and asymptotic freedom of QCD leaves exactly 3:
    the one free integer of the SM that a gauge theory calculates.
