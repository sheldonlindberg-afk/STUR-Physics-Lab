V4  Verdict
==============================================================================
   Census of underdense voids: 0.61-0.62 of the volume -> f_v0 = 0.64 today.
   Definitional systematic, both directions: VoidFinder keeps voids down to r = 10
   h^-1 Mpc, below Wiltshire's dominant ~30 Mpc-diameter scale (pushes f_v0 DOWN);
   it counts only galaxy-free spheres, missing low-density volume at void edges
   (pushes f_v0 UP).  The two VoidFinder runs disagree on the galaxy content (7%
   vs 18%), which sets the spread of the extrapolation in V3.
   Planck CMB (Duley+2013)              needs 0.695: +0.056 above the census
   SNe, Seifert+2024                    needs 0.737: +0.098 above the census
   SNe, official Pantheon+ (front 13)   needs 0.853: +0.214 above the census
   A void phase of 0.853 would sit at delta_v = -0.74 .. -0.50: from 'as empty as
   VoidFinder's voids' (Pan anchor) to 'half the mean density' (Douglass anchor).
   A void phase of 0.695 sits at -0.86 .. -0.66: as empty as the measured voids.
   VERDICT: the cosmic web supports f_v0 ~ 0.64-0.70, consistent with timescape's
   CMB value; Seifert et al.'s 0.737 is marginal; the official-Pantheon+ 0.853 is
   strained (needs voids much fuller than measured on one anchor), not excluded.
   LCDM mocks reproduce the same census, so this is a consistency test OF
   timescape, not evidence FOR it over LCDM.
