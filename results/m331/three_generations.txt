G1+G2  Generations allowed by the SU(3)_L anomaly and QCD asymptotic freedom
==============================================================================
   N_c = 3:  anomaly-free N_g = [3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18] ...   + asymptotic freedom: N_g = [3]
   N_c = 5:  anomaly-free N_g = [5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30] ...   + asymptotic freedom: N_g = [5]
   N_c = 7:  anomaly-free N_g = [7, 14, 21, 28, 35] ...   + asymptotic freedom: N_g = [7]
   -> N_g = N_c exactly: the generation number is LOCKED to the colour number.
      With N_c = 3: n = 2 anti-triplet + 1 triplet quark generations (the standard assignment).

G3  Combined with front 5
==============================================================================
   Witten / spin-charge twist: N_c odd.  3-3-1: N_g = N_c.  So the two 'threes' are one number;
   the measured N_g = 3 (LIT, LEP: N_nu = 2.98 +- 0.01) and the R-ratio (front 5) agree on it.
   Still open: why that one number is 3 rather than 5 or 7.

G4  Minimal 3-3-1 (beta = -sqrt3) vs data: the sin^2 theta_W = 1/4 wall
==============================================================================
   mu =     91.2 GeV:  sin^2 theta_W = 0.23129
   mu =   1000.0 GeV:  sin^2 theta_W = 0.24338
   mu =   2000.0 GeV:  sin^2 theta_W = 0.24693
   mu =   3000.0 GeV:  sin^2 theta_W = 0.24902
   mu =   4000.0 GeV:  sin^2 theta_W = 0.25051
   mu =   5000.0 GeV:  sin^2 theta_W = 0.25166
   sin^2 theta_W reaches 1/4 at mu_* = 3.63 TeV (one loop, Standard Model content)
   The U(1)_X coupling g_X^2 ~ s^2/(1 - 4 s^2) diverges there (Landau pole), so every new
   gauge boson of the minimal model must lie below mu_*.
   LHC 13 TeV dileptons (LIT, Nepomuceno-Meirose 2020): M_Z' > 3.7 TeV.
   Cs atomic parity violation (LIT, Long et al. 2019): M_Z' >~ 4.2-5.4 TeV.
   -> minimal 3-3-1 needs M_Z' < 3.6 TeV: at the LHC bound, and excluded by APV.
      Two-loop and threshold effects were NOT computed; evading APV needs them to
      raise mu_* by at least 16% (to 4.2 TeV).
   beta = +-1/sqrt3 variants have no such pole: the N_g = N_c result survives there,
   with M_Z' > 2.6-4 TeV (beta = -1/sqrt3, LHC 13 TeV) and >~ 2.9 TeV (beta = +1/sqrt3,
   8 TeV data) (LIT) - testable, not excluded.
