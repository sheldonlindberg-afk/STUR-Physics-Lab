Y3  The boundary condition: which measured inputs the selection depends on
==============================================================================
   At 4 TeV (SM running from M_Z): sin^2 theta_W = 0.2505, 1/alpha_em = 125.72,
   so 1/alpha_X = (1 - 4 s^2/3)/alpha_em = 83.73.
   All N_c are run from these measured couplings: the selection is conditional on them.
   Which s^2(4 TeV) would let each N_c reach the Planck scale (alpha_em, alpha_s fixed)?
   (a) Vary sin^2 theta_W(4 TeV) freely, keeping alpha_em and alpha_s at their measured values:
   beta = -1/sqrt3 no Yukawas  : N_c = 3 reaches Planck for every s^2 < 0.4489 (measured 0.2505, margin +0.1984)
        N_c = 5 reaches Planck only for 0.0662 < s^2 < 0.0740 (at best 1.5e+19 GeV): 3.4x below the measured value
   beta = -1/sqrt3 y_t, y_J = 1: N_c = 3 reaches Planck for every s^2 < 0.4512 (measured 0.2505, margin +0.2007)
        N_c = 5 reaches Planck only for 0.0637 < s^2 < 0.0809 (at best 2.2e+19 GeV): 3.1x below the measured value
   beta = +1/sqrt3 no Yukawas  : N_c = 3 reaches Planck for every s^2 < 0.2662 (measured 0.2505, margin +0.0157)
        N_c = 5 fails for every s^2 (at best 4.9e+14 GeV, at s^2 = 0.060)
   beta = +1/sqrt3 y_t, y_J = 1: N_c = 3 reaches Planck for every s^2 < 0.2720 (measured 0.2505, margin +0.0215)
        N_c = 5 fails for every s^2 (at best 6.9e+14 GeV, at s^2 = 0.058)
   Lowering s^2 relieves U(1)_X but makes SU(3)_L stronger, so N_c = 5 has at most a
   narrow band, far from the measured angle (beta = -1/sqrt3) or none at all (+1/sqrt3).
   (b) At the measured weak angle, what would rescue N_c = 5: a weaker electromagnetism.
   beta = -1/sqrt3: N_c = 5 needs 1/alpha_em(4 TeV) > 167.1 (y_t and y_J = 1); ours is 125.7: alpha_em 1.33x weaker
   beta = +1/sqrt3: N_c = 5 needs 1/alpha_em(4 TeV) > 234.4 (y_t and y_J = 1); ours is 125.7: alpha_em 1.86x weaker
   Reading: the selection is conditional on the measured electroweak couplings at the TeV
   scale. It does not predict them. Given them, only N_c = 3 stays perturbative to the
   Planck scale. In the beta = -1/sqrt3 variant, a world whose weak angle at the TeV
   scale were about three times smaller than ours would also allow N_c = 5.
