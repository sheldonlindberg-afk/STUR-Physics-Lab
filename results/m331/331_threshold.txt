   beta = -1/sqrt3 N_c = 3: 1.074e+30  1.074e+30  1.074e+30  1.074e+30  1.074e+30   5.1e-10
   beta = +1/sqrt3 N_c = 3: 1.195e+20  1.195e+20  1.195e+20  1.195e+20  1.195e+20   3.1e-10
   The N_c = 3 pole is independent of mu to 5e-10 in its logarithm: every sign and
   multiplicity in lambda is right, and the heavy content is exactly 3-3-1 minus the SM.
   Without thresholds the same check fails: 5.05e+19 vs 1.32e+20 GeV.
   N_c = 5 cannot pass it, and should not: below mu we run the real SM, with three colours,
   which is not 3-3-1 at N_c = 5 minus its heavy fields. This is the counterfactual boundary
   condition showing up directly: N_c = 5's pole moves by a factor 1.54 as mu goes
   from M_V/3 to 3 M_V. T3 takes N_c = 5's most favourable mu.
