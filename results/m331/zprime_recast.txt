Z7  Z-Z' mixing
==============================================================================
   The mixing angle is O(M_Z^2 / M_Z'^2) = 3e-4 at 5 TeV. Rotating the fermion couplings,
   g_Z' -> g_Z' cos(theta) - g_Z sin(theta), changes sigma x BR(ll) by:
   beta = +1/sqrt3, A: theta = +-1e-3 -> +0.15% / -0.15%
   beta = -1/sqrt3, A: theta = +-1e-3 -> +0.38% / -0.38%
   The W_L W_L and Z h decays that mixing opens are bounded by the 'everything' scenario
   of Z4 (equivalence theorem: they are the Goldstone components of the scalar triplets).
