L2  Which odd N_c survive perturbativity up to the Planck scale?
==============================================================================
   scalars = 3 triplets         : surviving odd N_c = [1, 3, 5]
   scalars = 3 triplets + sextet: surviving odd N_c = [1, 3, 5]
   (N_c = 1 has no strong force: front 5 excludes it.)
   -> THEORY now allows N_c = N_g in {3, 5}.  Five survives: b_L = +1/2 (barely
      asymptotically free) with triplets, and a far-away pole with the sextet.

==============================================================================
L3  How far is 5 from the edge?
==============================================================================
   N_c = 5         : b_L = +0.500  pole: none
   N_c = 5 + sextet: b_L = -0.333  pole: 2.7e+261 GeV (log10 = 261)
   One extra complex scalar triplet costs 1/6 in b_L; with 3 triplets, N_c = 5 loses
   asymptotic freedom after 3 more scalar triplets, but its pole would still sit far
   beyond the Planck scale.  Two-loop terms were NOT computed.
