Confining SU(3): area law is non-perturbative; no closed-form K is known for a
region pierced by a flux tube. The precise target that would complete U1+U2:
   compute the modular Hamiltonian / entanglement of a region cut by a confining
   string and check whether sigma_QCD appears as its boost-weighted energy,
   i.e. whether  dS_string = 2 pi sum beta(x) dE_string(x)  with E_string = sigma L.
Testable toy (next step): 2+1D compact U(1) or Z2 lattice gauge theory - confining,
small enough for exact diagonalisation.
