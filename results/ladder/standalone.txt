VERDICT
==============================================================================
   3-3-1 breaks to the Standard Model and then to electromagnetism: the photon, hypercharge,
   W, Z and Z' follow from the vacuum values. Chiral symmetry breaking gives the pions and the
   nuclear force. Electromagnetism, never broken, leaves the Casimir-Polder background between
   the diamonds. Gravity sits beside the chain: one-graviton exchange gives Newton's law and the
   experiment's 2.19 rad phase, and MacDowell-Mansouri writes GR + Lambda as SO(4,1) broken
   to Lorentz. Three links run across the rungs: rung 1's anomalies make matter neutral, which
   the diamond experiment needs to 2.8e-19 e per nucleon; its charges with rung 2's f_pi give
   the measured pi0 -> gamma gamma width; and the 3-3-1 cutoff, M_Pl, is rung 4's G. Inputs
   are measured; gravity's unification with the other forces is not derived. Rung 2's
   pion counting uses the linear sigma model, a stand-in for QCD, and rung 5 rewrites GR
   rather than going beyond it.
