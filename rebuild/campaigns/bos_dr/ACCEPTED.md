## What is and is not established

* **Established:** the complete bosonic one-loop Delta r in the on-shell scheme in 't Hooft-Feynman gauge, with every piece derived: the renormalization conditions, the vertex, box and wave-function diagrams, the cancellation of the UV and IR divergences, and the assembly; four builds and two audits with independent derivations.
* **Not established:** gauge independence of the total at general xi (only the Higgs-containing part was tested, campaign `higgs_dr_xi`; the 't Hooft-Feynman gauge total is what the SPEC fixes); the fermion-loop remainder, QCD and two-loop terms; the Z-box constants (signs and the Fierz factors 16 and 4) rest on the audit's own derivation and agreement with the published delta_VB, not on a check the gate has; that a build derived and did not recite (the gate cannot enforce it; the audit read both derivations); the provided `bos_se` is itself accepted (see its ACCEPTED.md).
* **Class counts:** R (orchestrator): 2 (fixed reference precision; the delta_vb wording); B: 0 (build b's reliance on private helpers: minor, fixed); C: 3 (the oracle's range in s^2, alpha and M_H; the Fierz ordering not named in the SPEC; the docstring); N: the Ward-identity step is now audited.
