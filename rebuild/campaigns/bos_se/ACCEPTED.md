## What is and is not established

* **Established:** the complete bosonic transverse self-energies of gamma, Z, W (and the gamma-Z mixing) at general k^2, including thresholds, large M_H up to 1e14 GeV and every MS-bar scale, with three independent routes agreeing (two blind builds, a hand derivation, a published formula set).
* **Not established:** the longitudinal parts (not exported); the self-energies with a photon mass (the lambda -> 0 limit is taken inside the loop functions, which is IR safe at the exported arguments); that a build derived and did not recite (the gate cannot enforce it; the audit read both derivations; build a's generator and build b's `gen/` scripts show the derivation); the two builds share the d-dimensional rules of the SPEC (conventional DR, MS-bar); M_H above 1e9 GeV is checked only at the oracle's points.
* **Class counts:** R (orchestrator): 1 (photon-field sign); B: 0; C: 2 (grid tolerance at exact zeros, Sigma^AZ wording); N: the oracle's "only the builds checked the non-Higgs gauge-boson terms" caveat is now removed by the audit's independent derivation.
