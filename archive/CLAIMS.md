# Claims ledger

*Every headline claim of this repository in one table: what kind of claim it is, the tests that pin
it, what it assumes, and what result would show it wrong. [`tests/test_claims.py`](tests/test_claims.py)
checks that every test named here exists, so a claim cannot outlive its evidence. Prior work for
each claim is in [NOVELTY.md](NOVELTY.md). Independent work, not peer reviewed.*

Labels:
- **CHECK**: an identity, theorem or count, verified by computation.
- **RESULT**: a numerical result under the stated conditions.
- **DATA**: a derived value against a measurement.
- **MODEL**: follows from a named, unproven model.
- **NEGATIVE**: a no-go, or a published idea that fails a test.
- **ASSESSMENT**: this repository's judgement of the literature.
